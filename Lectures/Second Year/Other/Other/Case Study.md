# Case Study

Case Study: Daniel – 5-Month-Old Infant Referred for Developmental Concerns
Referral Information
Daniel is a 5-month-old male infant referred by the GP following parental concerns regarding stiffness and intolerance of tummy time. The health visitor has also noted persistent head turning to the right.
Birth and Medical History
Born at 36 weeks gestation (late preterm)
Vaginal delivery
Birth weight: 2.5 kg
No NICU admission
No known medical diagnoses
Immunisations up to date
No reported seizures. No feeding difficulties. No significant illness since birth.
Family and Social Background
First child
Lives with both parents
No family history of neurological conditions
Parents report anxiety about development but are engaged and proactive
Parent-Reported Concerns
During subjective history, the parent reports:
“He feels stiff sometimes.”
“He really doesn’t like tummy time.”
“He startles easily, even when it’s not that loud.”
“He always turns his head to the right.”
“His hands feel tight.”
Parent reports Daniel becomes unsettled during prone positioning and tolerates it for less than one minute.
Developmental Information (Parent-Reported)
Smiles responsively
Brings hands to mouth
Limited reaching towards toys
No rolling observed
Head control improving but still wobbly when pulled to sit
Objective Assessment Findings (During Simulation)
Chronological age: 5 months
Corrected age: approximately 4 months
Observations:
Resting posture: head turned predominantly to right
Mild asymmetry in upper limb positioning
Primitive reflex assessment:
ATNR: strong, consistent right-sided response
Moro: exaggerated and easily triggered
Palmar grasp: strong bilaterally, slightly increased on right
Head righting: delayed and inconsistent
Limited tolerance of prone
No obvious visual tracking deficits. No obvious cranial deformity. No abnormal cry.
Clinical Questions for Students
What is Daniel’s corrected age and why does it matter?
Which reflex findings are expected, and which are concerning at this stage?
How do these reflex findings link to functional development?
What are your red flags, if any?
What is your provisional clinical impression?
What is your management plan today?
Is referral or escalation required? Justify your reasoning.
