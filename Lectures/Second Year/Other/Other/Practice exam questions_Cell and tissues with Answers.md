Provide a brief description of the role of Connective tissue, giving 2 examples
Answer to include mention of ‘Connective proper’, both loose and dense, including adipose tissue and elastic tissue, fluid connective tissue examples including the cardiovascular system and supporting type connective tissue, such as bone and cartilage.
Describe the main differences between the three types of muscle (cardiac, smooth, skeletal).
Cardiac muscles perform involuntary muscular movements of the heart, aiding development of filling and ejection of blood during the cardiac cycle. Skeletal muscles perform voluntary work to support and move the skeleton in locomotion and tasks of motion and dexterity. Smooth muscles enable involuntary movements of internal organs and blood vessels of the arterial system, functions including digestion, urination, and gas exchange at the lung.
