# Case 2 H&S Tasks

Physiotherapy Health & Safety Task
Scenario
You are working with a 6-week post-CABG patient attending cardiac rehabilitation. The patient presents with reduced exercise tolerance, mild shortness of breath on exertion, occasional non-cardiac chest discomfort, and anxiety about physical activity. Past medical history includes hypertension, type 2 diabetes, hyperlipidaemia, and a hip replacement. Baseline observations are stable.
Task
Identify the key health and safety considerations before, during, and after a physiotherapy session.
Part A – Pre-session Safety Checks
A1. Which baseline observations should be checked before commencing exercise?
A2. What symptoms would indicate the session should be postponed or modified?
A3. What advice would you give the patient regarding clothing, footwear, and hydration?
Part B – Safety During the Session
B1. What safety measures should be in place when conducting an exercise program for a post-CABG patient?
B2. How would you monitor intensity of exercise (consider subjective and objective measures)?
B3. What signs/symptoms would require the session to be stopped immediately?
Part C – Post-session & Environment
C1. What should you monitor in the recovery period following exercise?
C2. Why is it important to ensure adequate cool-down and gradual progression of activity?
C3. What infection control and manual handling considerations apply in a cardiac rehab class setting?
Part D – Reflection / Application
D1. How could the patient’s anxiety be addressed to promote safety and confidence in rehab?
D2. How might comorbidities (diabetes, hypertension, previous hip replacement) influence your safety planning?
D3. If the patient expressed chest pain during exercise, what would your immediate actions be?
