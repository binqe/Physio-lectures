# Prioritisation task

## Clinical Simulation Programme

## Student Workbook
Prioritisation Task
By the end of this session, students will have opportunity to:
Planning, organising and prioritising clinical workloads.
Develop an awareness of the impact of their own actions on themselves and others
Consider issues important for the management of own workload
Stevie Wonder – admitted with Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) 2 days ago, no discharge plan yet and medical team want him home by Thursday (It is Monday today).
Justin Bieber – admitted with a fall, not safe for discharge yesterday as impulsive and unsafe when mobilising.
Beyonce Knowles – has not been seen by physiotherapists for 4 days due to high caseload. Initially admitted with a Community Acquired Pneumonia (CAP) and reduced mobility.
Brad Pitt – Rehab patient – fall and fractured public rami, nil discharge plan in place currently as not established a safe transfer method.
Adele – needs to complete stairs prior to safe discharge, aiming home today
Madonna – for discharge home on Wednesday however needing to progress confidence with frame, community PT referral to be sent.
George Clooney – slow stream rehab following prolonged delirium, has been an inpatient for 6 weeks, rehab referral needs to be sent as soon as possible.
Tina Turner - Patient awaiting placement in a Residential Home (RH), for progression to independent with frame.
