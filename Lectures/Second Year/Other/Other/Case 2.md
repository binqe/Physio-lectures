Case Report  
Cardiac Placement 
 
Subjective Assessment 
Presenting Complaint: 
The patient attended to cardiac rehab 6 week post Coronary Artery Bypass Graft surgery (CABG). 
Patient is recovering well and says the wound is healing well with no complications. Feeling well 
today. 
Patent reports shortness of breath on exertion, reduced exercise tolerance. Reports fatigue and 
occasional burning pain in the centre of chest, which isn’t related to exertion and goes away quickly. 
Patient has been suffering with some anxiety since diagnosis as previous to this didn’t realise there 
was anything wrong, and the surgery had been a big life event for them.  
History of Presenting Complaint: 
The patient underwent an elective CABG procedure 6 weeks ago due to diagnosis of coronary artery 
disease. Prior to surgery, he experienced worsening angina over the past 3-4 months, which was 
unresponsive to medical management. Post-operatively, he had an uneventful recovery but reports 
feeling deconditioned and breathless with minimal activity.

Impression/Diagnosis: 
The patient is recovering well post-CABG and presents with reduced exercise tolerance, mild 
breathlessness, and post-sternotomy discomfort, limiting physical activity. Patient reported that 
burning chest pain was relieved by Gaviscon, likely not cardiac related. Patient would like to return to 
cycling, golf and being able to play with grandchildren. Patient would like to understand more about 
condition and how to manage. Patient will benefit from Cardiac Rehab Exercise and on initial
