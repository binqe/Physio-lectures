## Slide 7

- CASP = Critical Appraisal Skills Programme
- Designed for health and social care
- 10 questions, 3 domains
- What is the CASP Checklist?

## Slide 8

- CASP Section A – Research Aims
- Was there a clear statement of the research aims?
- Did the study clearly define what it was investigating and why?
- Is a qualitative methodology appropriate?
- Does the study use qualitative methods (e.g., interviews, focus groups) to explore lived experiences and perceptions?
- Was the research design appropriate?
- Was the chosen method (e.g., ethnography, case study, grounded theory)
- suitable for answering the research question?

## Slide 9

- CASP Section A: Research Aims
- Was the recruitment strategy appropriate?
- Were participants relevant to the study?
- Were inclusion/exclusion criteria well defined?
- Was the data collected in a way that addressed the research issue?
- Were interviews, focus groups, or observations conducted rigorously?
- Were data collection methods clearly explained?
- Has the relationship between the researcher and participants been adequately considered?
- Were researcher biases and influences acknowledged?
- Did the researchers reflect on how their role affected the data collection?

## Slide 10

- CASP Section B: What are the results?
- This section assesses how well the findings are presented and analysed
- Have ethical issues been taken into consideration?
- Was informed consent obtained?
- Were ethical guidelines followed (e.g., confidentiality, data protection)?
- Was the data analysis sufficiently rigorous?
- Was the data analysed systematically?
- Were themes clearly identified?
- Is there a clear statement of findings?
- Were results clearly presented?
- Are conclusions backed by data?

## Slide 11

- CASP Section C: Will the results help locally?
- This section determines whether the study is useful and applicable.
- How valuable is the research?
- - Do the findings contribute to existing knowledge?
- - Can the study be applied in practice (e.g., healthcare,
- education, policy)?

- Transferability (External Validity)
- The extent to which the findings can be applied to other settings or groups.
- Techniques to Enhance Transferability
- Thick Description
- Comparative Cases

- Ensuring the research process is 	consistent, logical, and repeatable over 	time.

- Audit Trail
