Other Scenarios 
Multiple Sources 
Recent studies have examined the effect that heat stress has on 
cognitive performance (Schlader et al., 2015; Trezza et al., 2015; 
Mazloumi et al., 2014). 
 
Same Author and Same Year 
Dietary nitrate has been found to reduce the oxygen cost of cycling 
and walking (Lansley et al., 2012a; Lansley et al., 2012b). 
Distinguish the sources by inserting a lower case letter after the year in-text 
and in the reference list. 
 
Author Citing Another Author 
The phenomenological approach is the most popular orientation 
used to study personality in sport & exercise psychology (Vealey, 
2002, cited in Weinberg and Gould, 2011, pp. 33).
