# Case-Report Writing Task

CASE REPORT WRITING TASK
Reflect on the patients you worked with during your most recent clinical placement and write a case report on one of those patient cases.
Instructions:
Choose one patient case that you were directly involved in managing.
Ensure all identifying details are anonymised.
Follow the structured format outlined below.
Submit your completed case report by 15:00 on the due date.
