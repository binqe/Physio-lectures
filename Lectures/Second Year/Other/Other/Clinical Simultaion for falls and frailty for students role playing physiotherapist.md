Patient Scenario 
Mrs XYZ is a 78-year-old patient referred to physiotherapy following three falls in 
the past six months. 
She has:  
• A history of frailty  
• Polypharmacy  
• Reduced quadriceps strength  
• Poor balance  
• Significant fear of falling  
• Reduced confidence with walking  
• Reduced functional mobility 
She currently:  
• Walks slowly indoors  
• Avoids going outside alone  
• Uses furniture for support  
• Reports feeling “unstable” when turning
