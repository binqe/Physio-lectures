# presentation guidance

## Slide 1

- PT5051 – assessment support

## Slide 2

- The two assessments
- You are delivery a 10 minute presentation as a group to:
- Review and evaluate two different health related podcasts chosen from the list below.
- You should discuss the credibility of the information; the target audience; the accessibility; and the key take home health messages.

## Slide 3

- Use the following sub-headings to structure your presentation
- A summary of the podcast – aims, target audience and subject.
- The scientific evidence base.
- The accessibility
- The key take home messages
- Communication style and behaviour modification techniques
- Guidance to which podcast you would recommend and why.

## Slide 4

- In researching the podcasts (1)
- Each of you should:
- Review the landing page of the podcast. This will help you to:
- Understand the target audience
- Think about the speakers who they use – do they have the appropriate credentials ?
- Look for any credible information – based on scientific evidence
- Look for information that may discredit the podcast – is it linked to the presenters own financial benefit for example or are they using information that is not grounded in science

## Slide 5

- In researching the podcasts (2)
- Each of you should listen to extracts of different podcasts. You don’t have to listen to the whole series, but enough information to discuss
- The evidence base, the target audience, the take home messages, the target audience, communication styles, any behaviour modification used.
- Discuss the credibility as a group.
- Use those discussions to inform your presentation.
- Then decide as a group which podcast you recommend and why.

## Slide 6

- You should all present for equal time
- Each of you should present for around 3 minutes – you could do this as a block, or integrated.
- The presentation should be cohesive, in a standard format and you should demonstrate your team working skills.
- You should all try to answer questions equally
- You will be allowed to exceed your time by 1 minute, only. Any longer and I will ask you to complete your presentation straight away.
