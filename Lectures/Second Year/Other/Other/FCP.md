## Page 4

What is a FCP?
●
A regulated HCP who has undertaken additional training 
and experience within a specified scope of practice
●
Example: MSK Physiotherapy, Dietetics, Occupational 
Therapy, Paramedics, Podiatrists
●
To be the first point of contact and assessment for a 
defined group of patients
●
Practitioners in FCP roles support people to get faster 
access to the right care without the need of referral 
from a GP
●
FCP work apart of a multidisciplinary team

## Page 5

What is a FCP?
●
Requires recognition through Health Education England
●
The physiotherapist MUST have completed a taught or 
portfolio route
●
The clinician must have a minimum of 5 years of post 
graduate experience (of which 3 in speciality area MSK)
●
FCP work at masters level in their clinical pillar of 
practice

## Page 6

What do FCP Do?
●Specialist independent ax, diagnosis and management of undifferentiated 
undiagnosed MSK conditions
●Provide expert advice, education and preliminary management which may 
include individualised exercise programs
●Refer for and interpret any necessary investigations 
●Refer for community/secondary care services (physio, hand therapy, 
orthopaedics, rheumatology etc)
●Promote health through lifestyle education
●Signpost to other services eg mental health, social prescribing
●MAY hold qualification to provide injection therapy
●MAY hold qualification to prescribe certain medications
