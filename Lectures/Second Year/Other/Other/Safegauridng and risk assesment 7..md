# Safegauridng and risk assesment 7

## Confidentiality Agreement
The Confidentiality Agreement sets out the responsibility of all students, staff, to protect private and sensitive information obtained during their involvement with Charlene B Z Enterprise Ltd or its partner organisations.
Purpose
The purpose of this agreement is to ensure that everyone understands the importance of maintaining confidentiality when dealing with personal, professional, or organisational information. This obligation helps to build trust, safeguard privacy, and comply with legal and ethical standards.
Scope
This agreement applies to all information acquired during placement or project activities, including but not limited to:
Client or service user details and records
Staff, student, and volunteer information
Partner or organisational data, reports, and correspondence
Meeting discussions, case notes, and internal communications
Confidentiality Responsibilities
All participants agree to:
Treat all personal and organisational information as strictly confidential.
Only access or share information when it is necessary and authorised.
Not discuss confidential information in public or with anyone not directly involved in the work.
Securely store any documents, notes, or electronic data.
Return or delete all confidential materials at the end of the placement or project.
Report any breach or concern about confidentiality to the placement educator, supervisor, or data protection lead immediately.
Exceptions
Confidential information may only be shared when legally required or to prevent harm — for example, in safeguarding, risk of harm, or criminal activity cases. Any disclosure must follow organisational and legal procedures.
Acknowledgement
By signing the Confidentiality Agreement, each student have read and understood the confidentiality policy.
Agree to always uphold the principles of confidentiality; and
Understand that any breach of confidentiality may lead to disciplinary action or termination of placement.
