# Summative Assessment briefing

Applied Sciences for Physiotherapy Practice (PT5050)
Assessment Briefing: Unseen Exam
2025/26 Academic Year
Module learning outcomes:
At the end of this module, students will be able to:
Demonstrate an understanding of the components of anatomy, physiology and psychology such as spinal and central nervous and cardiorespiratory systems, and the dynamic relationships between human structure and function.
Demonstrate knowledge of the biopsychosocial concepts that may be implicated in the experience of people with problems affecting the spine, central nervous system and cardiorespiratory systems.
Demonstrate knowledge of the effect of pathophysiological processes underlying common central neurological, cardiorespiratory, and spinal conditions seen in contemporary practice.
Utilise the knowledge of biopsychosocial model to explain the implications of pathological conditions affecting the spine, central nervous and cardiorespiratory in individuals and populations.
Summative unseen written examination
This will be a 2-hour paper focused on the knowledge and understanding of human anatomy, physiology, behavioural and clinical science underlying physiotherapy practice. It is aimed at exploring the breadth of knowledge taught in this module. It will contribute 100% of the module mark and assess LO 1, 2, 3, 4. Students will have an opportunity to practice written examinations before the summative assessment.
Section A: 60 multiple choice questions covering anatomy, physiology, and psychology/sociology; you will be expected to answer all questions in this section.
Section B: Eight short answer questions covering anatomy, physiology, and psychology/sociology components; you will be expected to answer all questions in this section.
If you require additional information about this assessment, contact the module lead.
Formative unseen exam questions
There will be some multiple-choice questions on Weblearn, which will be practice questions for the summative assessment. These will be based on content taught on the module so far. They will be marked immediately so feedback will be provided when complete. These must be attempted and over 40% must be scored, although the score does not contribute to the module grade.
Attendance requirement
This module has a practical attendance requirement of 80%, which is stipulated by The Chartered Society of Physiotherapists. Failure to meet this requirement will result in a competency based assessment, which is an additional exam, that will assess your ability to perform the skills in the classes that were missed. This is in place to help your learning and develop your skills as a competent practitioner.
