- Community Rehabilitation
- Community rehabilitation should be focused on maximising and maintaining independence and thereby reduce the overall burden on the healthcare system.

## Slide 9

- Intermediate Care
- Time limited, short term (ca 6/52) health and/or social care provided to adults who need support after D/C from acute and virtual wards to support rehab, reablement and recovery.
- Can be entirely health, entirely social care, or (ideally) an integrated service of both. They may be commissioned by the NHS and/or Local Authorities and delivered by providers across health, social care, housing, independent and voluntary sectors.
- (CSP 2025)

## Slide 10

- Intermediate Care Framework
- best practice guidance
- recommended actions for improving step down intermediate care
- using a new therapy-led community rehab model of care
- part of the Urgent & Emergency Care recovery plan
- (CSP 2025)

## Slide 11

- Intermediate Care Framework Aims
- Improve people’s outcomes and experience of hospital D/C
- Reduce hospital re-admissions
- Reduce avoidable/ premature long term care provision
- Improved patient flow and D/C from hospital settings
- Develop a new national standard for D/C, by improving and standardising community rehab data.
- (CSP 2025)

## Slide 12

- Intermediate Care Framework Pathways
- Pathway 0 – community-based assessment of need
- Pathway 1 – community-based assessment, home based therapy led interventions.
- Pathway 2 – assessment of needs, move to short term bedded care (may be followed by pathway 1)
- (CSP 2025)

## Slide 13

- Intermediate Care Framework Outcome (if implemented)
- improve rehab offer for 100s of 1000s of frail & elderly people currently being let down by the system & denied the opportunity to regain their independence after being in hospital
- (CSP 2025)

## Slide 16

- What is a LTC?
- An illness that cannot be cured but can usually be controlled with medicines or other treatments
- Chronic, reoccurring, & often complex, comprising more than one health issue.

## Slide 17

- Long-term Conditions
- Long term conditions are now a central task of the NHS; caring for these needs requires a partnership with patients over the longer term rather than providing single, unconnected “episodes” of care.
- Aids people w/ LTCs to:
- live well
- age well
- die well
- Five Year Forward View, NHS England 2014
