## Slide 2

- Introduction to the first podcast
- The BBC World Service “Health Talks” podcast (2000-2001) : this podcast talks about different healthcare issues abroad that range from global healthcare inequilities like the Cuban healthcare paradox which goes into detail about the trade embargo that was placed on cuba and the effect it had on public healthcare, public health policies which had to be created to counter the publics growing fear of pollution in Tehran and the effect it had on the rising rates of CRP conditions, CV conditions and other health related problems, and lastly social/environmental determinants of health which covers the rising obesity rates in california at the time and the various factors that contribute to the rise such as, the cultural dominance of fast food, sedentary lifestyles, “super sized portions” and other socioeconomic factors.

## Slide 3

- Introduction to the second podcast
- The ZOE Science and Nutrition podcast is about translating the latest health, nutrition, and gut health research into actionable advice for listeners. the podcast features top scientists who explain complex topics in an accessible way to help people improve their weight and overall health. Topics covered are wide-ranging, including things like blood sugar, inflammation, and the effects of different foods on the body.

- Communication Styles

- Factual segments
- Focus on global health issues and statistics
- ZOE Science & Nutrition
- Conversational and engaging

## Slide 5

- Behaviour Modification Techniques
- BBC Health Check
- Focused on informing and raising awareness
- Use experts to encourage reflection
- Provides public health guidance
- Less interactive
- Encourage change through awareness and fear appeals
- ZOE Science & Nutrition
- Motivational interviewing style
- Provides practical tips
- Encourages gradual, lifestyle modifications
- Frequent positive reinforcement
- Provides step by step guidance

## Slide 6

- Podcast Recommendations
- ZOE Science & Nutrition has a better communication style and approach for behaviour change
- Uses relatable dialogue
- Include practical strategies that encourage change
- BBC Health Check is good for awareness and credibility
- Less effective at motivating personal change

## Slide 7

- Credibility
- BBC Health Checks
- Produced by BBC
- Sources include World Health Organisation (WHO), medical experts, studies
- Strong credibility
- Limited motivating personal change
- Raises global awareness
- Less actionable advice
- ZOE Science & Nutrition
- Hosted by Jonathan Wolf
- Potential bias with commercial links and incentives (ZOE app, supplements)
- Frequently use academics
- Personalised claims may lack sufficient evidence
