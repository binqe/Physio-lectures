# Test your knowledge - Lecture 2

Test your knowledge
1. The COM-B model explains that behaviour is a result of interactions between:
A. Cognition, Observation, Motivation, Behaviour
B. Capability, Opportunity, Motivation, Behaviour
C. Confidence, Orientation, Mindset, Behaviour
D. Competence, Objectivity, Motivation, Belief
2. Which of the following best describes a Behaviour Change Technique (BCT)?
A. A psychological theory
B. A small, observable component of an intervention designed to change behaviour
C. A long-term treatment plan
D. A counselling session
3. Which of the following is an example of a “Capability” barrier in the COM-B model?
A. Lack of confidence
B. Lack of knowledge or skill
C. Limited time
D. Lack of family support
4. In the COM-B model, which factor relates to external circumstances that enable or prevent behaviour?
A. Opportunity
B. Capability
C. Intention
D. Motivation
5. Which Behaviour Change Technique involves setting a specific and measurable target with the patient?
A. Self-monitoring
B. Goal setting
C. Modelling
D. Feedback
6. Which of the following statements best describes “self-monitoring”?
A. Observing other patients performing the same behaviour
B. Recording and reflecting on one’s own actions or progress
C. Receiving feedback from a physiotherapist
D. Setting long-term goals
7. According to CBT, the interaction between thoughts, feelings, and behaviours is known as:
A. The Motivation Cycle
B. The Cognitive Triangle
C. The COM-B Wheel
D. The Learning Loop
8. In physiotherapy, using CBT principles means focusing on:
A. Diagnosing mental health disorders
B. Changing unhelpful thoughts that affect physical recovery
C. Prescribing medication for pain
D. Avoiding emotional discussions with patients
9. Which of the following is NOT part of the Behaviour Change Wheel?
A. Intervention functions
B. COM-B system
C. Policy categories
D. Physiological responses
10. Which of the following is an example of “Enablement” as a Behaviour Change Technique?
A. Teaching correct technique
B. Providing a gym membership
C. Offering education about anatomy
D. Giving positive feedback
11. Which communication approach reflects CBT-informed practice in physiotherapy?
A. “You have to do your exercises every day.”
B. “Let’s explore what makes it hard for you to do the exercises.”
C. “Just ignore the pain and push through.”
D. “I’ll do it for you next time.”
12. Which Behaviour Change Technique focuses on reinforcing progress with positive comments or rewards?
A. Education
B. Reassurance
C. Feedback and reinforcement
D. Environmental restructuring
13. According to ethical guidelines, physiotherapists using CBT techniques should:
A. Offer psychological therapy for depression
B. Stay within professional scope and refer complex cases
C. Avoid discussing patient beliefs
D. Focus only on physical symptoms
Answers
1. The COM-B model explains that behaviour is a result of interactions between:
A. Cognition, Observation, Motivation, Behaviour
B. Capability, Opportunity, Motivation, Behaviour
C. Confidence, Orientation, Mindset, Behaviour
D. Competence, Objectivity, Motivation, Belief
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: COM-B stands for Capability, Opportunity, and Motivation, which interact to produce Behaviour.
2. Which of the following best describes a Behaviour Change Technique (BCT)?
A. A psychological theory
B. A small, observable component of an intervention designed to change behaviour
C. A long-term treatment plan
D. A counselling session
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: BCTs are the smallest “active ingredients” of behaviour change interventions — specific, observable, and replicable techniques.
3. Which of the following is an example of a “Capability” barrier in the COM-B model?
A. Lack of confidence
B. Lack of knowledge or skill
C. Limited time
D. Lack of family support
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Capability refers to the physical and psychological capacity to engage in the behaviour.
4. In the COM-B model, which factor relates to external circumstances that enable or prevent behaviour?
A. Opportunity
B. Capability
C. Intention
D. Motivation
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Opportunity involves environmental and social factors that make behaviour possible or easier.
5. Which Behaviour Change Technique involves setting a specific and measurable target with the patient?
A. Self-monitoring
B. Goal setting
C. Modelling
D. Feedback
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Goal setting involves collaboratively agreeing on clear, achievable behaviour targets.
6. Which of the following statements best describes “self-monitoring”?
A. Observing other patients performing the same behaviour
B. Recording and reflecting on one’s own actions or progress
C. Receiving feedback from a physiotherapist
D. Setting long-term goals
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Self-monitoring involves tracking personal behaviour, such as exercise frequency or pain levels, to increase awareness and accountability.
7. According to CBT, the interaction between thoughts, feelings, and behaviours is known as:
A. The Motivation Cycle
B. The Cognitive Triangle
C. The COM-B Wheel
D. The Learning Loop
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: CBT is based on the Cognitive Triangle, showing that thoughts, emotions, and behaviours are interconnected.
8. In physiotherapy, using CBT principles means focusing on:
A. Diagnosing mental health disorders
B. Changing unhelpful thoughts that affect physical recovery
C. Prescribing medication for pain
D. Avoiding emotional discussions with patients
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Physiotherapists use CBT-informed communication to reframe unhelpful beliefs about movement and recovery.
9. Which of the following is NOT part of the Behaviour Change Wheel?
A. Intervention functions
B. COM-B system
C. Policy categories
D. Physiological responses
Correct Answer: D
Explanation: The Behaviour Change Wheel includes COM-B at the centre, intervention functions, and outer policy categories.
10. Which of the following is an example of “Enablement” as a Behaviour Change Technique?
A. Teaching correct technique
B. Providing a gym membership
C. Offering education about anatomy
D. Giving positive feedback
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Enablement removes barriers and provides resources or tools that make the desired behaviour easier.
11. Which communication approach reflects CBT-informed practice in physiotherapy?
A. “You have to do your exercises every day.”
B. “Let’s explore what makes it hard for you to do the exercises.”
C. “Just ignore the pain and push through.”
D. “I’ll do it for you next time.”
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: CBT-informed physiotherapy focuses on collaborative, reflective questioning and problem-solving.
12. Which Behaviour Change Technique focuses on reinforcing progress with positive comments or rewards?
A. Education
B. Reassurance
C. Feedback and reinforcement
D. Environmental restructuring
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Feedback and reinforcement acknowledge effort and progress, strengthening motivation and adherence.
13. According to ethical guidelines, physiotherapists using CBT techniques should:
A. Offer psychological therapy for depression
B. Stay within professional scope and refer complex cases
C. Avoid discussing patient beliefs
D. Focus only on physical symptoms
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Physiotherapists use CBT-informed approaches related to physical recovery, referring when deeper psychological issues arise.
