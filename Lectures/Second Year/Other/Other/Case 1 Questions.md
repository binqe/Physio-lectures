# Case 1 Questions

Case 1 Questions
1. Background & Subjective Assessment
1.1 What factors may have contributed to the patient’s fall at home?
1.2 Which elements of the social history are most relevant when planning discharge?
1.3 How might the patient’s age and comorbidities influence recovery following a rib fracture?
2. Objective Assessment
2.1 Why is it essential to assess airway and breathing in this patient?
2.2 Interpret the patient’s observations (SpO₂ 93% on room air, BP 130/90, HR 82). What do these values suggest?
2.3 What is the clinical significance of the patient’s inability to huff effectively during ACBT?
3. Functional Assessment & Rehabilitation
3.1 How does the patient’s current mobility compare with their baseline, and what does this indicate about functional decline?
3.2 Why was the use of a rotunda frame an appropriate intervention in this case?
3.3 What safety considerations must be addressed during mobilisation for this patient?
4. Problem List & Prioritisation
4.1 Identify the patient’s main problems and prioritise them. Justify the order of priority.
4.2 How might end-stage kidney disease and haemodialysis affect physiotherapy management?
4.3 What precautions should be observed when treating a patient with a rib fracture?
5. Planning & Prognosis
5.1 What short-term goals would be appropriate for this patient?
5.2 What discharge considerations need to be addressed before the patient returns home?
5.3 How might the patient and his wife benefit from referral to additional services (e.g., community physiotherapy, occupational therapy, social work)?
6. Critical Thinking / Reflection
6.1 If the patient were unable to clear secretions independently, what alternative strategies or interventions could be considered?
6.2 How would your management plan change if the patient sustained another fall during admission?
6.3 Reflect on your own learning: what knowledge or skills would you need to develop to manage this patient safely?
