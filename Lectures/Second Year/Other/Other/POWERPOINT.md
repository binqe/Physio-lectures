## Slide 10

- What is SMART
- Knee Pain – Strength Goal
- S – Specific:
- Do sit-to-stand exercises from the dining chair.
- M – Measurable:
- 10 reps, twice per day.
- A – Achievable:
- Uses a chair already at home; low equipment needed.
- R – Relevant:
- Improves knee strength and supports daily tasks like standing up.
- T – Time-bound:
- Every day for 14 days.
- SMART Goal:
- “Starting Monday, I will complete 10 sit-to-stand reps from the
- dining chair twice a day for 14 consecutive days.”
