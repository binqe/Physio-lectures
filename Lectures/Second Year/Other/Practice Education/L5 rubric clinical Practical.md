| Presentation, Structure, & Referencing (Transferable and Technical Skills) | 15% | Provides a thoughtful, critical, and insightful reflection demonstrating strong awareness of professional responsibility, risk management, and ethical duty to act in potential serious pathology. | Provides a clear and well-reasoned reflection with appropriate awareness. | Provides a reasonable reflection but limited depth or critical insight. | Provides a basic but accurate reflection with minimal elaboration. | Limited reflection with minimal development of professional responsibility. | Weak, unclear, or superficial reflection. | No reflection or unsafe mindset demonstrated. |

| Clinical Reasoning & Diagnosis | 20% | Demonstrates exceptional analytical reasoning, accurately interpreting findings and justifying diagnosis with evidence-informed clinical logic and high-level synthesis of information. | Demonstrates strong reasoning and accurate diagnosis with clear justification and appropriate linkage to assessment findings. | Demonstrates adequate reasoning with generally accurate diagnosis though justification may lack depth or integration. | Demonstrates basic diagnostic reasoning but limited clarity, support, or integration of findings. | Demonstrates weak or inconsistent reasoning with unclear justification of diagnosis. | Demonstrates significantly flawed reasoning, inaccurate interpretations, or unsupported conclusions. | No clinical reasoning demonstrated or diagnosis unsafe/incorrect. |

| Professionalism & Communication | 15% | Demonstrates exceptional professionalism, empathy, clarity, and adaptability in communication.
