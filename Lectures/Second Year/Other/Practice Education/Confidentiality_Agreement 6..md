# Confidentiality Agreement 6

## Confidentiality Agreement

### 1. Purpose
This agreement sets out the responsibilities of students undertaking community practice placements to ensure that all information about clients, staff, partners, and the organisation remains confidential.

### 2. Student Responsibilities
Treat all information about clients, staff, and the enterprise as strictly confidential.
Not disclosing any personal, medical, or sensitive information to anyone outside the placement setting, unless authorised or required by law (e.g., safeguarding concerns).
Store any notes, records, or placement materials securely.
Use information gained during placement only for learning purposes and not for personal or external use.
Always respect client privacy and dignity.
Report any concerns immediately to my placement supervisor.

### 3. Exceptions
Confidentiality may only be broken if:
- There is a safeguarding concern regarding a child or vulnerable adult.
- There is risk of serious harm to self or others.
- Disclosure is required by law.

### 4. Agreement
I understand that failure to comply with this agreement may result in removal from placement and notification to my university.
