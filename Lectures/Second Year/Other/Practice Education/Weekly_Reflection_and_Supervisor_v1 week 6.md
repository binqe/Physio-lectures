### 2. Activities Completed
(Briefly describe what you did this week — tasks, meetings, outreach, etc.)
___I reviewed the community noticeboard at Swiss Cottage Library and this helped me understand how local spaces can be used to promote health and financial information as I noticed that this library had only information about helping people financially. So having a poster there about a health event would be a different topic which may encourage people to attend.
I emailed Hornsey Vale Community Centre to ask if they could display information about the upcoming presentation.______________________________________________________
This helped me understand the structure of a health event, ensuring that the presentation is clear and easy to understand. Doing the slides on the healthcare roles I learnt more about how different roles such as GPs, Social Prescribers, Osteopaths and more  contribute to joint pain and self-management. Creating SMART goal slides helped me learn about how to break down goals into smaller steps that are easier for people to achieve.____________________________________________________
__What went well was that I was able to expand on the slides about the healthcare roles by including 6 roles that people may come across. Breaking the SMART acronym down makes it easier to follow. ________________________________________________________
_________A challenge I faced was trying to keep the slides visually engaging and avoiding putting too much text while still providing enough detail. Another challenge was not knowing how the audience would response. It’s a challenge not knowing whether the topics about roles in healthcare and SMART Goals would provoke a response from the audience._________________________________________________
