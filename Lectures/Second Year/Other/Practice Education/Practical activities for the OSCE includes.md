# Practical activities for the OSCE includes

Example of the practical activities for the OSCE includes:
ROM Assessment using the goniometer for the Shoulder, elbow, wrist, Hip, knee and ankle.
Strength and flexibility Assessment of the muscle groups around the joints of the UL and LL.
Neurological Assessment of the UL and LL including:
Dermatomes, myotomes and reflexes
Neurodynamic testing.
Special testing of some of the conditions that could affect the UL and LL.
Gait Assessment
Balance assessment
Functional Assessment
Manual therapy techniques such as mobilization of the peripheral joints and PNF
CRP assessment
Airway clearance techniques
