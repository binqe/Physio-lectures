# Clinical Placement Case Study

Clinical Placement Case Study
Patient Details
Initials: PB
Age: 98
Subjective Assessment
Presenting Complaint (PC)
Recent fall at home whilst showering. The patient was identified after a neighbour raised concern when he had not been seen leaving the house; emergency services were contacted. He sustained a right lower rib fracture and nasal bruising.
The patient reports a productive (“wet”) cough with retained secretions and mild shortness of breath, likely secondary to pain and reduced chest wall expansion following the rib fracture.
History of Presenting Condition / Past Medical History (HPC/PMH)
End-stage chronic kidney disease requiring haemodialysis (Monday/Wednesday/Friday)
Type 2 diabetes mellitus
Hypertension
No previous history of falls
Drug History
Furosemide
Amlodipine
Diazepam
Ibuprofen
Paracetamol
Vitamin supplements
Social History (SH)
The patient lives with his wife in a ground-floor flat with a single step at the entrance. There is no current package of care. His wife supports the majority of activities of daily living, including cooking and shopping. They are considering employing a cleaner.
He is retired and reports watching television as his main leisure activity.
Functional Baseline
Independent with indoor and community mobility using a walking stick
Occasionally rides a bicycle
Objective Assessment
General Observation
The patient was alert and lying in bed. Audible secretions were noted on coughing. Airway clearance was required prior to functional assessment.
A–E Assessment
Airway/Breathing: Patent airway, self-ventilating. SpO₂ 93% on room air. Audible secretions present.
Circulation: Blood pressure 130/90 mmHg, heart rate 82 bpm
Disability: GCS 15/15
Exposure: Dialysis access line in situ
Respiratory Assessment
The patient was able to perform the Active Cycle of Breathing Technique (ACBT) with moderate effectiveness but was unable to generate an effective huff.
Following coughing, secretions were cleared and breath sounds improved.
Functional Assessment and Mobility
Supine to sitting on edge of bed: independent
Sit to stand: required rollator frame (RF), close supervision (CSO1)
Mobilised approximately 10 metres with RF before requiring seated rest (wheelchair following)
Mobilised a further 10 metres back to bed with RF, CSO1
Returned to bed independently
The patient was left comfortable in bed with the call bell within reach.
Problem List
Retained airway secretions (currently manageable)
Mild shortness of breath
Right-sided rib fracture (pain impacting respiratory effort)
Reduced functional mobility compared to baseline
Frailty
Complex comorbidities (CKD on dialysis, T2DM, hypertension)
Clinical Impression
PB is a 98-year-old patient presenting following a fall, with a right rib fracture and associated respiratory compromise. He is currently functioning below his baseline level of mobility and demonstrates reduced exercise tolerance.
Observations remain within acceptable limits; however, the patient presents with borderline oxygen saturations, reduced airway clearance effectiveness, and increased fatigue. In the context of advanced age, frailty, and comorbidities, he is at risk of further functional decline.
