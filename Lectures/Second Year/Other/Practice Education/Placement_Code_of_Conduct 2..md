# Placement Code of Conduct 2

## Placement Code of Conduct
Place of Conduct Overview
The Place of Conduct refers to the environment or setting where professional, educational, or community activities take place. It represents the physical or virtual space in which students, staff, and volunteers are expected to demonstrate appropriate professional behaviour, communication, and ethical standards in line with organisational and university expectations.
Scope
The Place of Conduct covers all settings connected to the placement or community practice, including:
Community centres, health and wellbeing hubs, and outreach venues
Partner or host organisations
Online and digital platforms used for learning or client engagement
Remote or home-based working environments
Purpose
The purpose of defining the Place of Conduct is to ensure all participants understand the boundaries, responsibilities, and professional expectations associated with their role. Regardless of location, individuals are representing both the organisation and university, and should maintain a professional standard at all times.
Professional Expectations
Within the Place of Conduct, all participants are expected to:
Act with professionalism, integrity, and respect
Maintain confidentiality and adhere to GDPR and data protection policies
Follow health and safety procedures relevant to the environment
Promote equality, diversity, and inclusion
Communicate respectfully with colleagues, clients, and members of the community
Dress appropriately and present themselves in a professional manner
I agree to:
Attend placement on time and notify supervisor of absence.
Maintain professional behaviour and appearance.
Respect confidentiality of clients, staff, and community partners.
Follow all health & safety instructions.
Treat all individuals with dignity, respect, and fairness.
Engage positively in learning, feedback, and reflection.
