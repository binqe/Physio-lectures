# Data Protection 4

## Data Protection & Client Contact Statement
The Data Protection Overview outlines how personal and confidential information must be handled to ensure compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. It applies to all students, staff, and volunteers involved in placement or community activities under Charlene B Z Enterprise Ltd.
Purpose
The purpose of this section is to ensure that everyone understands their responsibilities when handling, storing, or sharing personal or sensitive information. This includes data belonging to clients, staff, students, and partner organisations.
Scope
Data protection applies to all forms of information, including:
Written records, notes, and reports
Digital files, emails, and electronic systems
Verbal communication and discussions involving personal details
Photographs, recordings, or online materials that identify individuals
Principles of Data Protection
All participants must follow the seven key principles of data protection:
Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency – handle information fairly and openly.
Purpose limitation – use data only for its intended purpose.
Data minimisation – collect only what is necessary.
Accuracy – keep information correct and up to date.
Storage limitation – do not keep data longer than required.
Integrity and confidentiality – store data securely and protect it from unauthorised access.
Accountability – be responsible for how information is managed.

### 1. Use of Personal Emails
- Students must not use personal emails, they can use separate accounts to contact partners or organization or university account, but not direct contact with patients without conformation of practice lead.
- No client contacts details (emails, phone numbers, addresses) will be stored on personal devices or shared outside secure systems.

### 2. Client Invitations to the Health Event
- Our enterprise will not directly collect or hold client personal data for the purpose of invitations.
- Instead, we will work with partner organisations (such as GP surgeries, community centres, charities, and social prescribing teams) to circulate invitations through their existing communication channels.
- Clients will therefore be contacted by trusted partners who already have a lawful basis to process their personal data.

### 3. Student Responsibilities
- Students must not record, copy, or store client personal details on personal devices.
- All communication with clients must go through approved partner organisations or supervised enterprise activities.
- Any breach of this policy must be reported immediately to the supervisor.

### 4. Benefits of This Approach
- Protects client privacy.
- Ensures compliance with GDPR.
- Reduces risk of accidental data breaches.
- Builds stronger partnerships with local organisations.
