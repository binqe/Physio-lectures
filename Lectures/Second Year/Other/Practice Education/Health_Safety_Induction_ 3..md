# Health Safety Induction 3

## Health & Safety Induction Acknowledgement:
Health and Safety Overview
The Health and Safety Overview outlines the responsibilities and procedures that ensure a safe and supportive environment for all students, staff, volunteers, and members of the community engaged in placement or project activities.
Purpose:
The purpose of this section is to promote awareness of health and safety standards within all practice settings, ensuring that everyone understands their role in maintaining a safe working environment. All individuals share responsibility for preventing harm, managing risks, and responding appropriately to incidents.
Scope
Health and safety apply to all placement locations and activities, including:
Community centers and outreach venues
Partner or host organizations
Community engagement
Online or remote working environments
Responsibilities:
All participants are expected to:
Familiarize themselves with the health and safety policies of the organization and any partner sites
Complete all required induction or safety training before starting activities when asked.
Report hazards, accidents, or near-misses promptly to a supervisor or placement coordinator
Follow safe working practices, including manual handling, infection control, and use of equipment
Ensure their actions do not put themselves, clients, or others at risk
Be aware of emergency procedures, including fire safety and first aid arrangements
Supervision and Support
Students will receive guidance from their placement educator or supervisor, who will provide health and safety information relevant to each environment. Regular supervision meetings are encouraged to discuss any concerns or incidents that may arise during the placement.
Commitment
Charlene B Z Enterprise Ltd is committed to maintaining high standards of safety and wellbeing. Everyone involved in placement or community activities is expected to uphold these principles and contribute to a culture of care and responsibility.
