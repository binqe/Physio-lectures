# OSCE Formative briefing and instructions

> ℹ️ **Year placement under review** — see `_REVIEW/Uncertain-Year`.

Formative OSCE Student Briefing
Objective Assessment of acute and chronic conditions
Aims
Assess the student's ability to demonstrate an understanding chronic pathophysiology, and ability to design, plan and execute physiotherapy assessment and a treatment.
Equipment
1. Plinth     2.  Two Chairs    3. Small table   4. a collection of various assessment and treatment tools such as goniometers, tape measures, cotton balls, neurotips, hammers, stethoscopes...etc.
Formative PT4000 and 7003 OSCE Instructions
Formative assessment is an opportunity to have a go at practicing your module assessments to evaluate your learning and understanding. This will enable you to identify your strengths and weaknesses to ensure success in the summative assessment at the end of module.
For this formative OSCE, you will have a 15-20 minute to prepare for objective assessment and treatment OSCE. There will be a 5-10 minute transition time between the preparation room and the assessment room.
During the OSCE, you will be presented with a case study of a patient with a chronic or an acute condition according to your timetable. Your task is to demonstrate appropriate assessment and treatment for this patient. The subjective information will already be provided to you, but you will perform the objective assessment as though you are meeting the patient for the first time.
OSCE Instructions:
You are allowed to bring your notes and paper into the assessment room.
Personal belongings, such as bags, mobile phones, and headphones, are not permitted in the assessment room.
Please arrive no more than 10 minutes before your allocated OSCE time.
Wear your placement attire, with shorts underneath, to allow you to demonstrate patient interaction within your group.
You will be assigned to a group of three students. Each student will rotate through the roles of assessor, student, and patient.
Each student will be randomly assigned by the staff member to be assessed in one case study in any of the taught core specialities- i.e. MSK, Cardiorespiratory or neuro physiotherapy.
You will have 15 minutes to complete the entire assessment, so be sure to manage your time effectively. You will have a member of staff monitoring the whole process.
As an assessor, you will be assigned to evaluate one of your colleagues based on a provided answer sheet. The answer sheet includes a variety of options inclusive of any answer that may be valid.
A staff member will observe and assist the assessor in evaluating your performance during the assessment and mark the student based on a rubric.
At the end of the session, you will receive feedback from your tutor regarding your performance.
Formative assessment grades are not part of the final assessment grade.
For MSK:
The assessment includes 2 main parts:
Prepare to conduct an objective assessment of the patient, including a Must, Should, Could table. Your assessor will select one specific assessment technique for you to demonstrate during the OSCE.
Prepare to demonstrate and explain treatment options for the patient, which should include:
One therapeutic exercise (with progression and regression options),
One manual therapy technique, and
One piece of health promotion advice.
You may find it helpful to create a problem list for the patient in your preparation.
Be ready to explain your clinical reasoning behind the selection of your assessment and treatment techniques. This should include relevant evidence to support your choices.
For Neuro:
Explain and Demonstrate how you would assess Neuro-physiotherapy assessment in this patient
Explain and Demonstrate assessment of balance function.
Explain and demonstrate how to improve the patient indoor mobility and independence.
For CRP:
How would you approach Assessing and treating your patient? You will be asked to demonstrate 2 of the assessment techniques as chosen by your assessor.
Create a problem list
Demonstrate one treatment technique and explain how it will help this patient (based on your assessment and problem list).
You might be asked some questions relevant to the case: questions may include your knowledge on the pathophysiology, and the secondary effect of the illness.
