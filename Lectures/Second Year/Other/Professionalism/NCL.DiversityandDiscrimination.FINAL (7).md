## Slide 7

- Glossary
- Unconscious Bias prejudice in favour of or against one thing, person or group compared with another. It can manifest in how we judge and evaluate others, or how we act towards members of different groups. We all have bias and stereotypes we cannot avoid it.
- Prejudice is pre-judgement about another person based on the social group(s) to which that person belongs. Consists of thoughts and feelings, including stereotypes, attitudes and generalizations that are based on little or no experience and then projected onto everyone from that group.
- Discrimination is action based on prejudice. These actions include ignoring, exclusion, threats, ridicule, slander and violence. Discrimination may be personal maybe internalised racism/misogyny/colourism, interpersonal, When a racial group’s collective prejudice is backed by legal authority and institutional control it is transformed into institutional and structural racism. Racism is a structure, not an event.
- Micro aggressions have been defined as brief and common daily verbal, behavioural, and environmental communications, whether intentional or unintentional, that transmit hostile, derogatory, or negative messages to a target person because they belong to a stigmatized group. Can result in othering, a sense of not belonging.

## Slide 18

- How would you respond to this?
- Step 1: Reiterate. Restate what you heard. This step enables you to reflect on what you have heard as opposed to what you think you may have heard. Repeating what you have heard limits miscommunication and misinformation.
- Step 2: Contemplate. Count to 10 before responding. Gives you time to think about your response / what you want to say. May also help to diffuse conflict
- Step 3: Breathe. We often hold our breath in difficult situations, so take a few breaths to help to settle emotions and thoughts
- Step 4: Communicate. Speak clearly and thoughtfully. Focus on the negative statement rather than the person who said it. Make it clear that you disagree with the statement

## Slide 19

- Finding the right words and phrases
- "It is not funny to me; it is a racist comment"
- "Racial jokes are unacceptable"
- "I have no idea if you realise the impact of those words"
- That sounds racist to me; if you continue to talk like that, I will have to leave"
- "We respect all people and do not tolerate those words  or phrases"
- "That comment has made me feel very uncomfortable"
- "I find what you just said offensive, can we talk about it please"
- “What do you think he would think if he heard you calling him that"?
