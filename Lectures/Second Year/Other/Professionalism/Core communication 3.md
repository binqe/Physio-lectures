- Risk of over/ under investigation

- Communication challenges :
- Reduction in therapeutic relationship
- Missed opportunity to pick up non -verbal cues
- Potential miscommunication/ misunderstanding
- Technological and practical issues
- Impact on physiotherapists:
- Potential increase in stress and anxiety
- Risk of isolation
- Challenges in balance of work-life
- Cons of remote/virtual consultations

## Slide 16

- Emergency or urgent communication: SBAR

**Notes:** Originally developed in the US military for communication on nuclear submarines

## Slide 17

- Briefly state the current situation or problem
- Who, What, When and Where
- E.g. I’m Samantha a student physio calling from Cedar ward. I’m calling about Mr Smith a patient who’s blood pressure keeps dropping.
- SBAR : SITUATION

## Slide 18

- Provide relevant context and background
- The presenting condition of the patient
- Any procedures/ operations
- How the pt condition has changed
- Observations and outline how they differ from their baseline
- E.g. Mr Smith came in for a heart operation 3 days ago. He has been having some low blood pressure when I stand him up. I measured his last blood pressure and it was 60 systolic. He was previously stable with no issues.
- SBAR : Background

## Slide 19

- Share you professional evaluation of the situation if you can or share your concern/ worry
- E.g. I mobilised with him yesterday and I am unsure what has caused this change in his blood pressure. It is not safe to mobilise him.
- SBAR : Assessment

## Slide 20

- State your suggestion for addressing the situation
- Establish if there is anything you can do in the meantime
- E.g. Please can you come and review Mr smith? Is there anything you would recommend I do whilst I wait for you ?
- SBAR : Recommendation

## Slide 25

- SOAP NOTES
- S : Subjective assessment
- Consent
- PC , HPC,  PMH, MH, SH
- Any updates from you or the patient
- O: objective assessment
- A:E assessment
- Risk assessment : how much help is needed for this patient , any special considerations.
- Special tests or outcome measures
- Treatment section ( Rx )
- What you did in your session
- A: Analysis
- Short summary of what you did/ found and any updates
- The clinical reasoning behind your treatment choices
- P: Plan
- What you plan to do with the patient in your next session
- Any goals
