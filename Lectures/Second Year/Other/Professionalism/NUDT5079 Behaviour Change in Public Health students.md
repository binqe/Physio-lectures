## Slide 3

- What do we want to achieve?
- Absence of disease?
- Medical model, longstanding.
- Health as wellbeing?
- Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity (WHO, 1948)
- Health as a resource?
- A resource for everyday life, not the object of living. A positive concept emphasizing social & personal resources as well as physical capabilities (WHO, 1986)
- New definition?
- Ability to adapt & self-manage in the face of social, physical & emotional challenges (Huber et al, 2011)

## Slide 4

- What is public health?
- ‘The science and art of preventing disease, prolonging life and promoting physical health and efficiency through organised community efforts’
- (Winslow, 1920)
- Population based
- Emphasises collective responsibility for health
- Recognises the key role of the state
- Emphasises partnerships with all those who contribute to the health of the population

## Slide 9

- Health Belief Model
- Likelihood of an individual taking action based on interaction between four types of belief:
- Am I at risk?
- Perceived threat
- It is a serious problem
- Making changes would help
- Outcome expectancies
- Changes are difficult
- Self efficacy
- (Simpson, 2015)
- Cues to action
- Self-efficacy
