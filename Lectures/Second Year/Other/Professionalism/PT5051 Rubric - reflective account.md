| Cognitive Skills | Applies knowledge and skills with outstanding creativity and independence. Synthesizes ideas from diverse sources to develop innovative solutions to complex problems. Exhibits exceptional critical analysis, evaluation, and synthesis of information. Challenges assumptions and demonstrates a high level of intellectual rigor and insight.  | Applies knowledge and skills effectively to address complex problems, demonstrating creativity and independence. Integrates ideas from diverse sources to develop well-reasoned solutions. Shows strong evidence of critical analysis and evaluation. Constructs well-reasoned arguments and demonstrates the ability to weigh evidence and draw balanced conclusions. | Applies knowledge and skills effectively to address problems, though with limited originality. Demonstrates the ability to integrate ideas from different sources. Shows clear evidence of critical analysis and evaluation. Constructs arguments and demonstrates the ability to weigh evidence and draw conclusions. | Applies knowledge and skills to address problems, though with limited effectiveness or originality. Demonstrates the ability to apply ideas from different sources. Shows some evidence of critical analysis and evaluation, though arguments may lack depth or sophistication. Draws conclusions based on evidence but may not fully explore alternative perspectives.  | Applies knowledge and skills to address straightforward problems, though with limited effectiveness or originality. Shows limited evidence of critical analysis or evaluation. Arguments are often descriptive rather than analytical, with conclusions based on limited evidence.  | Fails to apply knowledge and skills effectively to address problems. Demonstrates little ability to integrate or apply ideas. Shows little evidence of critical analysis or evaluation. Arguments are weak, unsupported, or irrelevant.  | Fails to apply knowledge and skills to address problems. Demonstrates no ability to integrate or apply ideas. Shows no evidence of critical analysis or evaluation. Arguments are absent, incoherent, or irrelevant.  |
| Research and Analysis | Conducts independent research with a high degree of methodological rigor. Demonstrates exceptional ability to analyze, interpret, and critically evaluate data and sources. Presents research findings perceptively, convincingly and appropriately in a wide range of formats, and has gathered, processed and interpreted a wide range of complex data efficiently and effectively.  | Conducts research using appropriate methodologies, with a good level of independence. Demonstrates the ability to analyze and interpret data and sources critically. Presents research findings perceptively, convincingly and appropriately in a wide range of formats, and has gathered, processed and interpreted a wide range of complex data efficiently and effectively.  | Conducts research using appropriate methodologies, with some independence. Demonstrates the ability to analyze and interpret data and sources. Presents thorough research findings perceptively and appropriately in a wide range of formats, and has gathered, processed and interpreted a wide range of complex data efficiently and effectively.  | Conducts research using appropriate methodologies, though with some limitations in scope or depth. Demonstrates the ability to analyze and interpret data, though with occasional errors or omissions. Consistently presents their research findings effectively and appropriately in different formats, and has gathered, processed and interpreted data efficiently and effectively. | Conducts research with some understanding of methodologies, though with significant limitations in scope or depth. Demonstrates basic ability to analyze and interpret data, though with frequent errors or omissions. Presents their research findings, in several formats, and has gathered, processed and interpreted data effectively.  | Fails to conduct research effectively or use appropriate methodologies. Demonstrates little ability to analyze or interpret data. Does not present research findings clearly or effectively, and their gathering, processing and interpretation of data is unsatisfactory.  | Fails to conduct research or use appropriate methodologies. Demonstrates no ability to analyze or interpret data. Research findings are not demonstrated clearly or effectively, and their gathering, processing and interpretation of data is very poor or misunderstood.  |
