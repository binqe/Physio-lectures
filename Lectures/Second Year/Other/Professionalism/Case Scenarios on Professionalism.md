# Case Scenarios on Professionalism

Placement Scenarios on Professionalism

| SCENARIO 1 | It is the first week of placement, a student is arriving late for placement up to 30 minutes on occasion. They are reporting that it is due to travel complications. However, on a number of occasions the student has not contacted the placement educator to inform them that they are running late. The educator has had to ring or text the student to ensure that they are on their way 
What are the main issues in this scenario? 
Do you think the student is behaving in a professional manner?
Why ? 
What is the solution as the educator ? What is the solution as the student? 
How should you approach travel problems when you go on placement ? |
| SCENARIO 2 | A student goes away for the weekend and becomes unwell. They have very limited signal and internet and they have not contacted their educator or their link tutor to let them know that they are unable to go to placement on Monday. When Monday arrives the educator and link tutor are not able to get hold of the student until 2pm, when the student contacts the link tutor on another family members phone. The link tutor then has to contact the placement site to inform them that the student is unwell and won’t be attending placement for the next 2 days whilst they recover. 
What are the main issues in this scenario? 
Do you think the student behaved in a professional manner? 
Why? 
What is the solution as the educator? What is the solution as the student? 
How should you approach sickness when you go on placement? |
| SCENARIO 3 | A student on an MSK placement is continually running out of time to write their notes. They decide to take the patient notes home to finish off at the end of the day. When the educator discovers that this is happening the placement is terminated. 
What is the issue in this scenario ? 
Do you think the student behaved in a professional manner ? 
Why 
Was this the correct outcome ? What is the solution for the student? 
How might you ensure that you manage your time appropriately on placement? |
