# Public Health Task

## Clinical Simulation Programme

## Student Workbook
Public Health Task
By the end of this session, students will have opportunity to:
Demonstrate an awareness of the wider determinants of population health
Identify and understand the impact of health inequalities within the placement setting
Promote a sense of inclusion and belonging, demonstrating anti-discriminatory behaviour.
Task 2:
Task 2 discussion points
Can you identify the factors that contributed to the health inequalities experienced by Bruce in this case study?
How do you think the covid pandemic could contribute to furthering health inequalities?
How would you address this if you were involved in his care?
How would you identify health inequalities within the placement setting?
How can you promote a sense of inclusion and belonging when you are in clinical practice?
Reflection Questions
What did you learn the determinants of population health? How would you apply this knowledge during your next placement?
