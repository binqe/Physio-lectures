# Case Study Briefs for PST Role Plays

Dietitian Brief
Service User Brief
You are a 28 year old customer care agent called Stacy Gould who has been referred for dietetic advice to aid weight management.  You have been diagnosed with obesity at your GP practice, you would like to get pregnant but the excess weight is reducing your fertility. This has made you frustrated and angry, why can’t you be like everyone else.
You can provide a diet history of your choice, but it must include the following:
Skips breakfast
Large intake of snack and food on the go
Eats out a lot as she lacks basic cooking skills
5+ fruit and vegetables daily
You live with your long term boyfriend who is also overweight and is supportive of this change.
You don’t do any physical activity, but not averse to trying something new as long as you don’t feel embarrassed.
Dietitian Brief
Service User Brief
You are a 67 year old retired man called Simon Cooper.  You currently live with your daughter, her partner and two grandchildren.  Your wife passed away last year and this has affected you badly; you have suffered from low mood and became apathetic.  This has resulted in weight loss and a lack of interest in eating.
Your GP referred you to the Dietitian to help increase intake and thus improve weight.  At the GP appointment you agreed to have four milkshake drinks per day, but you have found this overwhelming and also you are not very keen on milk based drinks.
You find that you suffer from appetite changes and experience feelings of fullness quickly.  People keep telling you, you are underweight, but don’t relate and can get upset at this.  You also find it difficult to mobilise secondary to low energy levels.
You can provide a diet history of your choice, but it should include the following:
Erratic meal pattern; minimal amounts eaten
Managing about ½ a milkshake drink per day
Daughter helps prepare meals, but reports he feels overwhelmed by a big plate of food
Dietitian Brief
Service User Brief
You are Vikram Patel a 48 year old taxi driver.
You live with your wife and two children.
You were recently diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and have been referred to the dietitian for healthy eating advice. You are quite apathetic with the diagnosis as it doesn’t cause any symptoms.
You are aware that you have obesity but your weight has never been discussed with a health care professional and you have never linked your weight to the diabetes.
You can provide a diet history of your choice but it must include the following:
3-4 soft drinks daily
Large portions, especially carbohydrates such as rice, ciabattas and roti served with curry.
Fond of sweet foods as eats these daily.
Uses full fat dairy products
You have limited activity.
