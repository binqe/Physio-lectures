## Slide 16

- Other structures
- Joint capsule
- - surrounds the GHJ
- - dense fibro-cartilage
- - very lax - allows ROM, no role in support
- Articular cartilage
- - hyaline cartilage that lines the humeral head
- and glenoid cavity, also the AC and SC joints
- Glenoid labrum
- - fibrocartilage ring that attaches to the
- articular cartilage of glenoid fossa
- - deepens the socket

## Slide 17

- Glenoid Labrum
- fibrocartilage ring that attaches to the articular cartilage of glenoid fossa
- deepens the socket and increases the surface area (acting as a static stabiliser of the GHJ)
- resists anterior and posterior movement, and assists with blocking shoulder dislocation and subluxation at the maximal ROM
- can be easily damaged acutely (e.g. dislocation) or by repetitive microtrauma (e.g. overhead athletes) - most common SLAP lesion (superior labral anterior posterior, at insertion of biceps tendon)

## Slide 20

- Biceps Brachii
- The tendon of long head of the biceps brachii also acts as an elastic ligament
- It strengthens the upper part of the articular cavity
- During contraction of the deltoid, it prevents the head of humerus from pushing superiorly against the acromion
- Holds head of the humerus in contact with the glenoid cavity i.e. preventing displacement
