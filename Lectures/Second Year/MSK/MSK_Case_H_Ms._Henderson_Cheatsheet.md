Case H — Ms. Henderson
19F · Competitive Gymnast · 8-month sharp LBP at L5, extension-provoked | Working Dx: Spondylolysis at L5 (Pars Interarticularis
Stress Fracture)
Target: 70%+ | Musculoskeletal (MSK) Physiotherapy
I PROBLEM LIST
• Painful and restricted lumbar extension
• L5 pars interarticularis stress fracture (structural integrity
compromised)
• L5 spinous process tenderness on palpation
• Potential underlying bone density compromise (RED-S risk)
• Paraspinal muscle guarding/spasm secondary to pain
• Unable to perform gymnastics skills requiring extension (walkovers,
back handsprings, beam skills)
• Reduced training capacity and skill progression
• Potentially affecting school/social life due to 8-month duration
• Psychological impact: identity as an elite athlete threatened by injury
I RED FLAGS
• Cauda Equina Syndrome Significance: High-grade spondylolisthesis
can compress cauda equina — saddle anaesthesia, bilateral leg
weakness, bowel/bladder dysfunction Action: Immediate A&E;
referral; urgent MRI
• High-Grade Spondylolisthesis (>Grade II) Significance: >50%
anterior slip of L5 on S1 risks L5 nerve root compression and spinal
instability; surgical intervention may be required Action: Urgent spinal
surgeon/orthopaedic referral; cease sport immediately; MRI + X-ray
(lateral standing view)
• Progressive Lower Limb Neurological Deficit Significance: Suggests
nerve root or cord compromise from listhesis or associated disc injury
Action: Refer to spinal team; cease rehabilitation; urgent imaging
