▸Intrathoracic pressure changes inversely with volume of
thoracic cage

▸Diaphragm relaxes
▸Rib cage contracts
▸V→P
▸Air passively forced out of lungs

▸Complain of pain localized to the thorax or costal cage
▸Varies with respiration
▸Aggravated by twisting, pulling, pushing, or lifting, especially 
during overhead activities
▸History of trauma to thoracic spine or ribs

RESPIRATORY RIB DYSFUNCTION

▸Often painless

SUBJECTIVE HISTORY: DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSIS
SUBJECTIVE HISTORY: DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSIS
▸CAUTION!!! The thoracic spine is the most common site of referred pain 
within vertebral column
▸Thoracic pain may originate from all of the viscera
▸Systemic illnesses, such as rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, 
malignancy, and other conditions which cause pain referral must be included in 
the differential diagnosis
