# Clinical Simulation Outcomes (MSK and Neuro Physiotherapy)

## Page 1

Clinical Simulation Outcomes (MSK & Neuro Physiotherapy) 
By the end of the clinical simulation session, students will be able to: 
Assessment & Clinical Skills 
• 
Conduct a structured subjective assessment for MSK and neurological patients 
• 
Perform a focused objective assessment including posture, movement, strength, balance, and 
gait 
• 
Identify key impairments relevant to MSK (e.g. posture, mobility, pain) and Neuro practice (e.g. 
weakness, tone, balance, gait) 
Clinical Reasoning 
• 
Link patient history, posture, and movement findings to symptoms 
• 
Demonstrate clinical reasoning for both MSK and neurological presentations 
• 
Identify red flags and apply safe decision-making 
Treatment Planning 
• 
Formulate appropriate treatment goals based on assessment findings 
• 
Select and justify initial management strategies for MSK and Neuro patients 
• 
Adapt interventions based on patient response and safety 
Communication & Professional Practice 
• 
Communicate clearly and confidently with patients 
• 
Explain assessments and exercises in patient-friendly language 
• 
Demonstrate professional behaviour, consent-taking, and safety awareness 
• 
Reflect on performance and receive constructive feedback
