# Shoulder Joint case study part 2

Shoulder joint case study
Part 2
Clinical Examination:
Observation: normal shaped cervical curve, normal alignment of the shoulder joint, observed mild scapular dyskinesis, no visible swelling, scars  or muscle atrophy at the neck, shoulder or elbow.
Palpation: Tenderness over the supraspinatus tendon and subacromial space; mild tenderness at the lateral epicondyle of the elbow. No tenderness over CA joint, or SC joints
ROM: Full active ROM in flexion (pain free). Active shoulder abduction: 0-180° (painful arc from 60-120°), external rotation: 0-45° (with discomfort), internal rotation: hand reaches L3 level. Full passive elbow range of motion (0-145° flexion, 0° extension). Full active ROM in cervical spine (Pain free).
MMT: Shoulder flexion 4/5, abduction: 3+/5, external and internal rotation strength: 3/5 (both with pain). Elbow flexion: 5/5, elbow extension: 5/5. Pain on resisted wrist extension (4-/5 strength).
Special Tests:
Shoulder: Positive empty can, Hawkins-Kennedy, and Neer’s tests.
Elbow: Positive Cozen’s and Mill’s tests for lateral epicondylalgia.
Cervical Screening: Negative Spurling’s test, no neurological deficits.
