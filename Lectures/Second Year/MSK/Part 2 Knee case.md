# Part 2 Knee case

## Page 1

Case Study 1 
Part 1 
Mary Thompson is a 62-year-old retired schoolteacher who reports 
persistent knee pain that has gradually worsened over the past few 
years. The pain began about 4 years ago, initially after a day of 
gardening, and has progressively gotten worse. Mary experiences 
worse pain in the morning after getting out of bed, which somewhat 
improves throughout the day but intensifies again by evening. 
Prolonged walking, climbing stairs, and kneeling aggravate the pain, 
while rest and elevation provide some relief. Over-the-counter 
acetaminophen helps manage the discomfort. Mary’s former job 
involved standing and walking for long periods, and although she 
enjoys gardening, she has limited exercise outside of this. She is 
slightly overweight and has no history of significant knee injuries. 
Currently, she only uses acetaminophen occasionally for pain relief 
and has never undergone any knee surgeries. Mary lives alone in a 
single-story house and has a supportive network of friends and 
family who assist with chores. Her goal is to manage her pain 
effectively and maintain mobility to continue gardening. 
Part 2
