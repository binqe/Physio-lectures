Subjective Assessment
Occupation? A lot of standing? Extensive Walking? 
Or Trauma (Direct or indirect)? Mechanism of 
Injury?
Is the joint swollen? Does the swelling occur 
with activity or several hours after activity, or 
does the joint feel tight at rest?
Has the ankle been injured before, or does it 
have any feeling of weakness?
Does the knee “give way?
Was there a “pop” when the injury occurred?
For active patients what type of workout they 
practice? Terrain or surface? Warm up and 
cool down?
Does the patient notice any deformity at the time of injury or after? Was the patient able to 
continue activity afterwards?
Type of shoes they usually wear?
