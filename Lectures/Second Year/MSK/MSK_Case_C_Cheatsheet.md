I PROBLEM LIST
• SIJ pain right-sided (VAS 6/10)
• Reduced lumbopelvic motor control
• Ligamentous hypermobility (postpartum)
• PSIS tenderness on palpation
• Possible pelvic floor weakness
• Unable to perform single-leg yoga postures
• Difficulty rolling in bed / disrupted sleep
• Pain exiting a car (asymmetric load)
• Unable to teach / demonstrate yoga fully
• Baby care tasks (lifting, feeding positions)
I RED FLAGS
None identified

—
I DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSES
1. Lumbar Radiculopathy (L5/S1)
• Low back and buttock pain referral pattern
• Could worsen with sustained postures
• Supporting: buttock pain + occasional leg symptoms in some patients
2. Hip Joint Pathology (FAI / Labral Tear)
• Groin/buttock pain in a flexible, high-demand athlete
• Could be aggravated by yoga postures (extreme ROM)
• Supporting: young active female, demanding hip positions
