Diagnosis/Impression
The patient’s symptoms appear to be related to post-stent placement complications, potentially nerve irritation or inflammation. The limited ROM and neck stiffness could be secondary to guarding due to pain. While musculoskeletal involvement is likely, the potential for cardiovascular involvement requires further investigation. A multi-disciplinary approach is recommended for management, including input from a cardiovascular specialist. |
