Case D
Mr. Rossi — 45M, Amateur Marathon Runner | 3-month deep left buttock pain · "Tight hamstrings" · Occasional cramp to knee ·
Worse: hard chair sitting, initial running phase
Target: 70%+ | Musculoskeletal (MSK) Physiotherapy
I PROBLEM LIST
• Deep gluteal pain left-sided
• Piriformis/deep external rotator overload
• Sciatic nerve mechanosensitivity
• Tenderness deep to gluteus maximus
• Pain on resisted hip abduction (seated)
• Possible hip internal rotation restriction
• Unable to run comfortably (initial phase pain)
• Cannot sit on hard chairs comfortably
• Likely impaired marathon training plan
• Possible impact on work (prolonged sitting)
• Recreational activity restriction
I RED FLAGS
None identified
I PATHOPHYSIOLOGY
—
I DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSES
1. L5/S1 Lumbar Radiculopathy
• Buttock pain radiating to leg in a runner (disc loading with high-impact sport)
• Leg pain with cramping quality
• Supporting: 45-year-old male (age-related disc degeneration possible)
2. Proximal Hamstring Tendinopathy
• Ischial tuberosity / buttock pain in a runner
• Worse sitting on hard surfaces (ischial tuberosity compression)
• Onset with increased training load
