Proliferation

1.RESOLUTION – Dead cells & 
cellular debris are removed
2.REGENERATION – Damaged tissue 
is replaced by cells of same type
3.REPAIR – Original tissue is replaced 
with scar tissue
Two processes of repair;
1. FIBROBLAST FORMATION
2. ANGIOGENESIS

## Page 37

Immobilisation?
• Complete immobilization of the knee for 
an extended period is generally 
contraindicated because of the prolonged 
stiffness, muscle atrophy, and chronic 
pain that result
• Indications for the use of a knee 
immobilizer include the acute (or 
presurgical) management of: 
• Quadriceps rupture
• Patellar tendon rupture
• Medial collateral ligament rupture
• Patellar fracture or dislocation
• Limited number of other acute traumatic knee 
injuries
