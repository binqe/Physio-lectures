## Slide 14

- The reticular formation and raphe nuclei
- Major source of serotonin in the brain
- Numerous functions, including mediation of cardiovascular and respiratory functions, involvement in awareness (sleep, consciousness, alertness, habituation), pain modulation and maintenance of muscle tone, balance, and motor coordination.
