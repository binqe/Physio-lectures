## Slide 5

- Hypothetico-Deductive Model (HDR)
- Process:
- 1. Gather data
- 2. Generate hypotheses
- 3. Test and confirm/refute
- 4. Reach reasoning outcome
- Advantages:
- Logical and structured
- Disadvantages:
- Time-consuming for novices
- Example: Stroke patient with weakness → hypothesise UMN lesion → test and confirm via imaging.

## Slide 6

- Pattern Recognition
- Used by experienced clinicians.
- Quickly matches patient presentation to known 'illness scripts'.
- Example: Recognising Parkinsonian gait pattern.
- ✅ Quick, efficient
- ⚠️ Risk of bias if pattern incomplete

## Slide 7

- Narrative Reasoning
- Focuses on patient's story, emotions, and values.
- Informs shared decision-making and motivation.
- Example: Young adult with MS anxious about independence → goals include emotional resilience and function.

## Slide 8

- ICF Framework in Neurology
- International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF):
- Body functions/structures → e.g., spasticity, weakness
- Activity → e.g., walking, transfers
- Participation → e.g., work, social life
- Environmental factors → e.g., home adaptations
- Supports holistic and goal-oriented assessment.

## Slide 9

- Reasoning Focus Types
- Impairment-focused: Address specific deficits (e.g., strength, tone)
- Activity-focused: Improve task performance (e.g., transfers)
- Participation-focused: Enhance life roles (e.g., work, community reintegration)
- Balanced reasoning integrates all three.

## Slide 10

- Applying Models to Neurological Cases
- Stroke: HDR for lesion localisation; ICF for function goals.
- Parkinson’s Disease: Pattern recognition in gait; narrative for independence goals.
- •Multiple Sclerosis: Distinguish relapse vs progression; narrative supports coping.
- Spinal Cord Injury: Impairment (tone), Activity (transfers), Participation (community).

## Slide 11

- Neurological Care Pathways (UK)
- Stages:
- 1. Acute care – Stabilisation and early MDT involvement
- 2. Inpatient rehab – Intensive, goal-directed therapy
- 3. Community services – Home-based, adaptive strategies
- 4. Long-term management – Maintenance, education, relapse prevention

## Slide 12

- Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) Roles
- Physiotherapist: Mobility, function, recovery
- OT: ADLs, environmental adaptation
- SLT: Communication, swallowing
- Neuropsychologist: Mood, cognition
- Nurse: Education, monitoring
- Social worker: Support access
- GP/Consultant: Medical management

## Slide 13

- Barriers & Facilitators to Access
- Barriers:
- Socioeconomic inequality
- Geographical variation
- Long waiting lists
- Facilitators:
- Early referral, MDT integration
- Tele-rehab, home visits
- NICE guideline standardisation

## Slide 14

- Evidence-Based Practice in Neurology
- Integrates:
- 1. Research evidence
- 2. Clinical expertise
- 3. Patient values
- Example: Evidence-based gait training tailored to patient’s lifestyle and goals.

## Slide 15

- Reflection

- Evidence
