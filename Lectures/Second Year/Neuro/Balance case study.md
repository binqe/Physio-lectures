# Balance case study

Case Study 2
Mrs C is a 74-year-old woman who lives in a small bungalow with her husband. She has a history of recurrent falls because of poor balance. She can achieve sit to stand with the assistance of 1 and once standing can ambulate with a rollator frame for about 2 metres. She has muscle strength of power 2 and 3 in both ankle and knee muscles respectively. She wants to be able to walk independently with a rollator frame.
What subjective information would you like to gather about this client? Identify key areas.
Give 3 examples of questions you would use to begin to identify the patient’s main problems and expectations of physiotherapy
Devise and demonstrate your initial objective assessment of this patient including at least one validated outcome measures of balance or gait?
What might you expect as possible findings?
Devise an exercise regime for Mrs C and demonstrate three possible exercises to address her problems. Include at least two specific balance exercises. Identify how you would adapt your exercises to the home environment, for Mrs C to practice alone. Produce a short advice sheet on carrying out the exercise safely when on her own, to act as a reminder.
Describe and demonstrate how you will progress these exercises and explain your rationale.
You are working with a Physiotherapy Assistant (PA). What aspects of your role could you delegate to the PA and what instructions would you give them? Include advice on when to seek the input of a qualified Physiotherapist
