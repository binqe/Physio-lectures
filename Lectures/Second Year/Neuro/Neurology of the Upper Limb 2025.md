## Page 28

Organisation of the brachial plexus
• BRACHIAL PLEXUS
• Roots, trunks, divisions, cords and branches
• Formed by 5 ROOTS
• Anterior rami of the lower four cervical and the first 
thoracic nerve roots (C5 – C8, T1)
• They form brachial plexus after giving off their 
segmental supply to the muscles of the neck
• The roots merge to form TRUNKS
• TRUNKS
• Upper (C5&C6)
• Middle (C7)
• Lower (C8, T1)
• Each trunk divides into anterior and posterior 
divisions (six divisions)
• DIVISIONS re-group to form three cords (anterior, 
lateral and posterior (relative to their position to the 
axillary artery)

## Page 30

Organisation of the brachial plexus - 2
• CORDS
• Posterior cord is formed from the 
three posterior divisions of the trunks 
(C5-C8, T1)
• Lateral cord is the anterior divisions of 
the upper and middle trunks (C5-C7)
• Medial Cord is the continuation of the 
anterior division of the lower trunk 
(C8, T1)

• Muscles of the forearm

• Flexor carpi ulnaris

profundus
• Hand, it supplies:
• Thenar muscles: 
• Two lateral lumbricals (index and 
middle finger

• the palmar lateral three and half 
fingers
• Lateral surface of the palm and wrist
