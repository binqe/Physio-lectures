## Page 5

Drug History 
Medications:
Find out their medications, dosage, schedule. 
Are they effective? 
ON/OFF SYMPTOMS 
ON - Effects of medication
OFF - Emergence of PD ssx. 
1.
“Wearing off” is characterized by the re-emergence of Parkinsonian symptoms, usually three to four hours after 
a dose. 
2.
Unpredictable “off” periods
3.
Freezing of gait (FOG).
4.
Failure of the “on” response.
