| Instructions for Students
You are tasked with formulating a treatment plan for this patient who has experienced a left-hemisphere ischemic stroke resulting in right-sided hemiparesis. Use your clinical reasoning skills and knowledge of NHS physiotherapy practices to guide your decision-making. The treatment plan should focus on improving functional mobility, addressing the patient's impairments, and involving a multidisciplinary team (MDT) as required.
Steps to Follow:
Review the Subjective and Objective Assessments: Summarize the key findings from the case report, focusing on the patient's symptoms and the effects on functional mobility. Consider the implications of hemiparesis, speech difficulties, and dysphagia on the treatment plan.
Formulate the Problem List: Based on the subjective and objective assessments, identify the key impairments limiting the patient’s functional ability.
Curate Your Treatment Plan: Develop a holistic treatment plan with short-term and long-term goals. Use NHS resources and guidelines to plan your interventions and consider the input from MDT members such as speech and language therapists, occupational therapists, and nursing staff. |
