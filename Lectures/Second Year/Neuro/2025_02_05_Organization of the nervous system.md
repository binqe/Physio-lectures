## Slide 19

- Cells of the nervous system - Microglia
- Resident immune cells of the nervous system
- Sentinel/physiological role in surveillance of the nervous tissue
- Role in neuronal development and synapse refinement
- Similar to macrophages (belonging to the myeloid lineage)
- Can be activated during neuronal damage and inflammation (e.g. Alzheimer’s disease)

## Slide 20

- Cells of the nervous system - Astrocytes
- Support cells for the neurons
- Form the blood brain barrier
- Delivery of nutrients to the neurons
- Limited access from the bloodstream
- Interact with synapses
- Modulation of synaptic transmission
- Influences in inflammatory situations
- Neurodegenerative diseases
