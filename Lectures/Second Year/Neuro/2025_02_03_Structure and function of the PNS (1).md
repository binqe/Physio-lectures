## Slide 10

- Nerve plexuses
- Spinal nerves can intermingle into plexuses
- Neuropathies of the plexuses can have atypical symptoms
- Cervical plexus
- C1-C4
- Phrenic nerve (C4) controls the diaphragm (motor)
- Brachial plexus
- C5-T1 (sometimes C4, rarely T2)
- Divided in superior (C5-C6), middle (C7) and inferior trunks (C8-T1)
- Innervation of extensor and flexor muscles of the arm
- Lumbosacral plexus
- L2-L4 (Lumbar plexus), ventral and medial thigh muscles
- L4-S3 (Sacral plexus), dorsal thigh, all leg and foot muscles and gluteal muscles

## Slide 15

- Sympathetic and parasympathetic systems
- The parasympathetic system regulates “resting” autonomous body functions (e.g. digestion, renal secretion, etc.)
- The sympathetic system regulates “fight-or-flight” responses (e.g. pupil dilation, increased hearth beat, bronchial dilation, sweating, etc.)
