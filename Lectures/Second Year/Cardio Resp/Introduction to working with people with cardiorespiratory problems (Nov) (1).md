## Slide 17

- Definitions
- Hypoxia- Low levels of oxygen in the tissues (SpO2< 94%)
- Hypoxemia- Low levels of oxygen in the blood (PaO2<10.6mmolHg)
- Hypercapnia- Raised levels of carbon dioxide (PCO2 >6mmolHg)

## Slide 23

- Wheeze
- A wheeze is a continuous, coarse, whistling sound produced in the respiratory airways during breathing.
- Heard on expiration.
- If same sound on inspiration then it is called “stridor”
- Present in may different diseases for different reasons
