I TREATMENT PLAN
I Airway Clearance: Active Cycle of Breathing Technique (ACBT)
• Full technique:
• BC (4–6 relaxed breaths) →
• TEE (3–5 deep breaths with 3-second inspiratory hold) → FET/Huff (1–2
• huffs at mid-volume, 1 at high volume) → BC. Repeat 3–4 cycles. Perform
• upright seated or in high side-lying. Aim for twice daily — morning on
I Breathing Exercise: Pursed-Lip Breathing (PLB)
• Breathe in through the nose for
• 2 counts. Breathe out slowly and gently through pursed lips for 4
• counts. PLB maintains positive pressure in the small airways on
• exhalation, preventing dynamic collapse — particularly important in
• obstructive disease where airways are prone to collapse during forced
I Exercise & Mobility: Graded Walking Programme
• Starting level:
• Begin at a pace
• maintaining Borg ≤4/10 and SpO2 ≥88%. For a high-BMI patient with likely
• moderate COPD, target 10–15 minutes of continuous walking at a
• comfortable pace. Time the session to follow morning ACBT and inhaler
I Smoking Cessation — Motivational Interviewing Approach
• Assess readiness:
• "On a scale of 0 to
• 10, how important is it to you to quit smoking, and how confident are
• you that you could if you decided to?" Explore what would shift the
• number upward — identify ambivalence, not resistance.
