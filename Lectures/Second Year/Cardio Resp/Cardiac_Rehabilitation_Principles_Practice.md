# Cardiac Rehabilitation Principles Practice

## Slide 1

- Cardiac Rehabilitation
- Principles & Practice

## Slide 2

- Definition
- Structured programme of exercise, education and risk factor modification
- Aims to improve cardiovascular health and quality of life

## Slide 3

- Core Principles
- Individualised exercise prescription
- Multidisciplinary approach
- Risk stratification and safety
- Patient education and self-management

## Slide 4

- Phases of Cardiac Rehabilitation
- Phase 1: Inpatient (early mobilisation)
- Phase 2: Early outpatient
- Phase 3: Structured exercise programme
- Phase 4: Long-term maintenance

## Slide 5

- Exercise Prescription (FITT)
- Frequency: 3–5 days/week
- Intensity: Moderate (40–70% HRR)
- Time: 20–60 minutes
- Type: Aerobic + resistance training

## Slide 6

- Monitoring During Exercise
- Heart rate, blood pressure, SpO₂
- Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE)
- Symptoms: chest pain, dizziness, SOB

## Slide 7

- Physiological Benefits
- ↑ Cardiac efficiency
- ↓ Resting heart rate
- ↓ Blood pressure
- ↑ Exercise tolerance

## Slide 8

- Clinical Benefits
- Reduced mortality
- Reduced hospital admissions
- Improved functional capacity
- Improved quality of life

## Slide 9

- Safety Considerations
- Stop if chest pain, severe breathlessness
- Monitor for arrhythmias
- Adjust for medications (e.g. beta-blockers)
