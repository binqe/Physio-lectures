## Slide 3

- 2D image of the lungs, heart, bones and vasculature of the thorax
- Bone= white
- Tissue = Grey
- Air = black
- 99% of the lung is air
- 1 CXR = 0.1mSv
- What is a Chest X-ray (CXR)

**Notes:** As radiation passes through the body the energy from the x-rays is absorbed at different rates by different parts of the body giving them a different appearance: 
Bone: white ( high absoption )
Tissue Grey ( medium absorbtion )
Air : Black ( low absoption) 

"A single chest x-ray exposes the patient to about 0.1 mSv. This is about the same amount of radiation people are exposed to naturally over the course of about 10 days."

## Slide 4

- Done in standing
- X-ray plate in front of patient’s chest
- Hands on hips
- Full inspiration
- Gold standard of CXR
- Heart appears stretched and longer
- Types of CXR : Posterior –Anterior (PA)

## Slide 14

- Mediastinum comprises the  heart, left and right main bronchi, major blood vessels, and trachea
- Look for
- Size : should fit in one side of chest
- Shape
- Position: 1/3 R , 2/3 L
- Borders and angles: Cardio-phrenic
- Not fixed therefore can be pushed or pulled
- Cardiac

- Short note on the Naso-gastric tube

## Slide 41

- Points to remember
