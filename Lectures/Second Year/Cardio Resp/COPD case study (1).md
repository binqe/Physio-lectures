# COPD case study

November 2024 – COPD Case Study:
Mr Koff (65-year-old male)
Physiotherapy assessment completed during annual COPD review:
Subjective Ax:
Presenting complaint: 		↑SOB with ↓Exercise capacity (slowly worsening over last 2/12)
Dx: 				COPD – diagnosed 5 years ago.
2 admissions via A&E in the last 12 months with an IECOPD
Rx with IV Abx/Steriods
Cough: 	Expectorates approx. x2 teaspoons of thick yellow sputum in the morning. This has been consistent & patient reports unchanged. Hasn’t been taught ACT previously.
Exercise capacity: 	Can walk approx. 20m on the flat before becoming SOB and needing to stop to rest, used to be able to walk 100m. No mobility aids.
Smoking Hx: 			Smoked approx. 20 cigarettes for 40 years = 40 pack year history. Gave up 8 years ago.
PMH: 				HTN, Hypercholesteremia, #tibia 2020 after falling downstairs
DH: 				Salbutamol, Symbicort, Atorvastatin, Ramipril, paracetamol PRN
Up to date with covid & flu vaccinations
SH: 				Lives with his partner in a house with stairs.
Works part-time as a mechanic.
Independent with ADLs
Objective Ax:
Observations:	Visibly SOB walking into clinic. Sitting in a forward lean position with arms fixed on knees. Thin. Clubbing visible on fingernails.
SpO2 88% on room air at rest
RR 24 BPM
BP 126/87
HR 96
Weight: 55KG Height 175cm BMI 17.9
Ausc:	AE throughout. No added sounds.

1 week after your assessment, Mr Koff attends an assessment for pulmonary rehabilitation. His partner explains his breathing has become worse over the last 24 hours. Mr Koff has started coughing up thick green secretions and is unable to speak in a full sentence before needing to take a breath. He started taking a rescue pack 12 hours ago (a broad spectrum Abx and steroids) that had been provided by the GP, but this hadn’t improved his condition. He appears distressed by his breathing, which is further worsening his presentation.
On examination, Mr Koff’s resting saturations were 84% on room air, with a respiratory rate of 30. Auscultation of his chest revealed widespread crackles throughout. His cough is wet and productive of thick yellow/green secretions. His partner is happy to take him home to look after him there.
