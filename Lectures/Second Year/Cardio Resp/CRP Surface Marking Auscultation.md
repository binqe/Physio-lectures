Date:   Tue 12/11/2024

Learning Outcomes: 
 
• Outline the surface markings of the lungs and lung fissures and pleura 
• Use a stethoscope to perform auscultation 
• Describe normal breath sounds to peers 
• Record normal breath sounds in clinical note form 
 
 
Identify the (palpable) Bony landmarks :  
 
Anterior:  
1.  Clavicle  
2. Suprasternal notch  
3. Manubrium  
o 
including the manubriosternal joint ( angle of louis)  
4. Sternoclavicular joint  
5. Body of sternum  
6. Xiphoid process / Xiphisternum  
7. Coracoid process 
8. True ribs ( 1-7)  
9. False ribs ( floating ribs )  
10. Costal cartilages  
11. Costal margins

• 
Skin to stethoscope not through clothes  
• 
Listen to all lobes  
• 
Compare both sides
