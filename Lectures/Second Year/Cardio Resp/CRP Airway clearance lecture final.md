## Page 8

AIRFLOW AND POISEUILLE’S LAW 
• THE FLOW OF GAS OR LIQUID THROUGH A TUBE 
• VARIABLES : 
• DIAMETER OF THE TUBE 
• SPEED OF FLOW THROUGH THE TUBE 
• LENGTH OF THE TUBE 
• VISCOSITY OF LIQUID/GAS IN THE TUBE 
• IN AIRWAY CLEARANCE: CHANGE OF ABOVE VARIABLES MAY 
IMPACT THE EFFECTIVENESS OF SPUTUM CLEARANCE

## Page 13

EQUAL PRESSURE POINTS
• A PINCH POINT WHERE AIRWAY PRESSURE 
MATCHES PLEURAL PRESSURE
• BEYOND THIS POINT A POINT OF COLLAPSE 
HAPPENS
• THIS HAPPENS IN CARTILAGES SUPPORTED 
AIRWAY IN NORMAL HEALTH INDIVIDUALS
• CAN BE AUGMENTED AND UTILIZE TO 
SUPPORT AIRWAY CLEARANCE

## Page 14

COLLATERAL VENTILATION
• STANDARD ROUTE OF AIRFLOW INTO THE ALVEOLI : 
TERMINAL BRONCHIOLES
• COLLATERAL VENTILATION CHANNELS:
•
CHANNELS OF LAMBERT (BRONCHIOLE TO ALVEOLI)
•
CHANNELS OF MARTIN (BRONCHIOLE TO BRONCHIOLE)
•
PORES OF KOHN (ALVEOLI TO ALVEOLI)
• ALLOW AIRFLOW PAST A BLOCKED BRONCHIOLE TO AID 
CLEARANCE
• NORMALLY NOT UTILISED IN HEALTHY INDIVIDUALS
• COMPLETELY DEVELOPS AT AROUND AGE OF 4

## Page 15

INTERDEPENDENCE 
• ALVEOLI ARE CONNECTED TO ONE ANOTHER
• IF ONE ALVEOLI STARTS TO COLLAPSE THE OTHERS 
WILL STRETCH AND HELP TO KEEP THEM OPEN -
ENHANCED TRACTION FORCE WHICH TRANSMITS TO 
ADJACENT ALVEOLI
• COUNTERACTING EARLY COLLAPSE OF AIRWAYS
• THIS RELIES ON THE ELASTICITY OF THE ALVEOLI AND 
THE LUNGS
• E.G ATELECTASIS

## Page 16

PENDELLUFT FLOW
• A CONCEPT REFERS TO THE MOVEMENT OF AIR 
BETWEEN AREAS OF LUNG DUE TO UNEVEN 
VENTILATION
• AIR MOVES FROM FASTER FLOW TO SLOWER 
FLOW AREAS
• CREATION OF EXTRA SHEARING FORCE
• HOMOGENISE VENTILATION

## Page 19

MOBILITY AND EXERCISE
• THE MOST SIMPLE WAY OF INCREASING AIRWAY CLEARANCE
• PATIENTS ARE IN A GOOD POSITION TO IMPROVE LUNG VOLUMES 
(UPRIGHT)
• INCREASES TIDAL VOLUMES NATURALLY WITH EXERCISE
• INCREASED MINUTE VENTILATION
• WORK OF BREATHING INCREASES EXIT FLOW -> SHEAR FORCE
• GET THEM SLIGHTLY BREATHLESS!
• SHOULD BE USED PRIMARY AS PREVENTATIVE NOT A RESCUE 
METHOD

• OFTEN USED IN THE CF/PCD POPULATION
• UTILISES THE EQUAL PRESSURE POINT THEORY

EVACUATE

## Page 24

GRAVITY-ASSISTED POSITIONING
•
ONE OF THE OLDEST METHOD USED
•
IDEA IS TO EXPLOIT THE USE OF GRAVITY TO FACILITATE SLIDING OF 
MUCUS FROM PERIPHERALS TO CENTRAL AIRWAY
•
NEWER STUDIES WITH RADIOACTIVE AEROSOL MARKINGS HAVE 
SUGGESTED GRAVITY ALONE IS UNLIKELY ABLE TO PROMOTE MUCUS 
DISPLACEMENT
•
IT IS SUGGESTED THAT THIS INSTEAD PROMOTES VENTILATION IN 
TARGETED AREAS
•
CONSIDER RISK FACTORS – HEAD RUSH, REFLUX, DISCOMFORT, 
INCREASED WOB SECONDARY TO DIAPHRAGM AND HEART 
DISPLACEMENT
•
NOT TO USE ALONE

SHAKING AND VIBRATIONS
• NORMALLY COUPLED WITH POSITIONING
• HANDS ARE PLACED ON THE PATIENT'S CHEST
• SHAKING AND VIBRATIONS APPLIED DURING 
EXPIRATION PHASE OF BREATHING
• AIM TO CREATE OSCILLATION ON EXPIRATORY 
FLOW TO DISLODGE THICK MUCUS
• SHAKING : SLOW FREQUENCY
• VIBRATIONS: HIGH FREQUENCY

## Page 28

PERCUSSIONS
• DONE WITH CUPPED HANDS. REPEATED 
RHYTHMICAL FIRM PATS ON CHEST WALL

• DURING TIDAL VOLUME
• FREQUENCY BETWEEN 4.6-8.6HZ
• EFFECTIVENESS IS DEPENDENT ON CHEST 
WALL COMPLIANCE

## Page 31

PEP: POSITIVE EXPIRATORY PRESSURE 
• STENTING OF AIRWAY
• INCREASE FUNCTIONAL RESIDUAL CAPACITY  AND PREVENT 
EARLY COLLAPSE
• UTILISATION OF COLLATERAL VENTILATION
• MOST EFFECTIVE AT PRESSURE OF 10-20CMH2O
• STUDIES SHOWS THE NEED TO REACH TARGETED PRESSURE FOR 
3-4 SECS PER BREATH TO BE EFFECTIVE
• CREATES BACK PRESSURE IN THE AIRWAYS DURING EXPIRATION 
• ESPECIALLY USEFUL IN UNSTABLE AND REACTIVE AIRWAYS
• HOWEVER LIMITS EXPIRATORY FLOW BIAS

## Page 33

ACT ADJUNCTS : OSCILLATING PEP
•
DEVICES THAT PROVIDE PEP AND OSCILLATION
•
SPLINTS OPEN AIRWAYS AND PROVIDES SHEARING FORCE TO AID 
IN SECRETION DISLODGEMENT
•
ACAPELLA/AEROBIKA : CAN BE USED IN MULTIPLE POSITIONS AND 
RESISTANCE CAN BE ALTERED, ALSO CAN ENTRAIN IN OTHER 
DEVICES
•
FLUTTER : GRAVITY DEPENDENT
•
BUBBLE PEP: MAINLY USED IN PAEDIATRICS AND POST SURGERY. 
TUBE PLACES IN WATER, PATIENTS THEN BLOW BUBBLES. THE 
AMOUNT OF WATER DICTATES THE RESISTANCE AND THEREFORE 
THE PEP. CAN BE LIMITED BY ASPIRATION RISKS IN PAEDIATRICS
•
EVIDENCE SHOWS EFFECTIVENESS AT 11-15HZ

IPPB
•
IPPB : INTERMITTENT POSITIVE PRESSURE BREATHING.
•
POSITIVE PRESSURE IS PROVIDED BY THE MACHINE DURING INSPIRATION 
•
EXPIRATION IS PASSIVE
•
INCREASES FRC, COLLATERAL VENTILATION AND INTERDEPENDENCE
•
DELIVERED VIA MOUTHPIECE OR FACE MASK
•
3 MODIFIABLE FEATURES :
•
POSITIVE PRESSURE
•
SENSITIVITY
•
FLOW CONTROL
•
CAN ADD IN ADJUNCTS SUCH AS PEEP VALVE AND OPEP.
•
NEED TO ENTRAIN 0.9% SALINE NEBULISER SOLUTION TO AVOID DRY AIR
•
STARTING TO BE PHASED OUT AS MACHINES ARE NO LONGER BEING REPAIRED OR 
SERVICED
•
CAN BE REPLACED BY CLEARWAY 2 COUGH ASSIST
