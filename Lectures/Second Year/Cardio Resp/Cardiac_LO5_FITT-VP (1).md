# Cardiac LO5 FITT-VP

## Slide 1

- LO5: Cardiac Rehabilitation – FITT-VP & Adaptations
- Principles for Exercise Prescription in Cardiac Populations

## Slide 2

- FITT-VP Principles
- F – Frequency: 3–5 days/week (aerobic), 2–3 days/week (resistance)
- I – Intensity: 40–70% HRR, RPE 11–14 (moderate)
- T – Time: 20–60 minutes/session
- T – Type: Aerobic (walking, cycling) + resistance training
- V – Volume: Total weekly energy expenditure (MET-min/week)
- P – Progression: Gradual increase based on tolerance and response

## Slide 3

- Aerobic Training (Cardiac Rehab)
- Continuous or interval training
- Warm-up and cool-down essential (5–10 mins)
- Monitor HR, BP, SpO₂, symptoms
- Avoid sudden intensity spikes
- Use RPE when on beta-blockers

## Slide 4

- Resistance Training
- 2–3 sessions/week
- Light to moderate intensity (40–60% 1RM)
- 1–3 sets of 10–15 reps
- Avoid Valsalva manoeuvre
- Focus on large muscle groups

## Slide 5

- Physiological Adaptations
- ↑ Stroke volume and cardiac efficiency
- ↓ Resting heart rate
- ↑ Peripheral oxygen extraction
- ↓ Blood pressure
- Improved endothelial function

## Slide 6

- Clinical Benefits
- Improved exercise tolerance
- Reduced symptoms (dyspnoea, fatigue)
- Reduced hospital admissions
- Improved quality of life
- Reduced cardiovascular mortality

## Slide 7

- Safety & Monitoring
- Stop if chest pain, dizziness, severe SOB
- Monitor vitals before, during, after exercise
- Individualise prescription
- Consider comorbidities and medications
