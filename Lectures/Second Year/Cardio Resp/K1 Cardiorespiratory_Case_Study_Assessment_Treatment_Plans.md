## Slide 8

- Clinical Reasoning and Prioritisation
- • Identify primary problem list
- • Prioritise immediate vs. long-term goals.
- • Apply SMART goal setting.
- • Modify plans according to response and comorbidities.

## Slide 9

- Outcome Measures
- • 6MWT distance and recovery.
- • MRC Dyspnoea scale.
- • Sputum volume and colour.
- • Quality of Life (SGRQ, CAT score).
- • Patient satisfaction and adherence.

## Slide 10

- Summary
- • Integrate comprehensive assessment into decision-making.
- • Tailor interventions to individual pathophysiology.
- • Monitor progress with measurable outcomes.
- • Reinforce education and self-management.
