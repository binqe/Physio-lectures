What is

What is 
physical 
activity?
Physical Activity
According to the World Health Organisation (WHO), physical activity is any bodily 
movement produced by skeletal muscles that requires energy expenditure
Physical activity can be categorised into occupational, sports, conditioning, and 
household 
Exercise
The term “exercise”, is a subcategory of PA.
Planned, structured, repetitive form of activity that aims to improve or maintain 
one or more components of physical fitness
For conditioning and improving the function of any part of the body
Physical rehabilitation
Physical rehabilitation aims to restore movement and physical function towards 
fitness. .
Dietz Model of rehab
preventative
restorative
supportive
palliative

## Page 6

Why is it 
important in 
cardiorespiratory 
patients in 
particular?
High prevalence of comorbidity in people with respiratory disease that is 
influenceable with exercise
Hypertension
40% in COPD patients vs 27% in all adults
Skeletal muscle dysfunction 10-15% loss of fat free mass in mild-mod COPD, up to 
50% in severe
Osteoporosis
50% in COPD patients; 30% in women / 20% in men
psychological disturbance
42% in COPD
Disordered sleep
10%
T2DM
12% vs 9% in normal
Renal insufficiency
22% vs 10% in general population
GORD
60% vs 20% in general population of high income countries

Why is it 
important in 
cardiorespiratory 
patients in 
particular?
Modulates elements of the disease itself
Evidences support that exercise is able to modulate inflammatory

leukotriene pathways

Strengthens immune response therefore reduce infection risks

What if you 
don’t maintain 
physically

Deconditioning is the decline in physical function of the body as a 
result of physical inactivity.
Atrophy of muscle bulks
Change in fibre types to more type 2 fast twitch
Reduced capillaries density
Worsening cardiac output
Reduced peripheral vascular tone
Stiffer and more effortful movement
Reduced bone density
Increase risk of injury

## Page 19

How do we 
test and 
measure 
exercise
Cardiopulmonary Exercise Testing
Gold standard exercise testing
Able to provide comprehensive information on cardiac response, 
perfusion, respiratory
Bruce protocol
Can be completed on treadmill, bike or even other instruments
Can complete with other investigations in conjunction e.g laryngoscopy
Incremental shuttle walk test / Beep test
6 minute walk test
Commonly used in oxygen prescription
Chronic respiratory disease monitoring
Transplant assessment
Has a learning effect
1 minute sit-to-stand test
Easy to complete
Good use to monitor trend
Less time consuming

## Page 20

How do we 
test and 
measure 
exercise
Calculation of maximal heart rate
220 – age
Easy to calculate
Inaccurate by 7 to 11 bpm
Overestimates in under 30s, underestimates in over 45s
Varies between fitness level and type of activities

Patient reported scale on effort of exertion
Costless and easy to use
Can be used by patient independently to pace physical activities
Newly developed Dalhousie Dyspnoea and Perceived Exertion

## Page 23

Pulmonary 
rehabilitation
A comprehensive intervention based on a thorough 
patient assessment followed by patient-tailored therapies 
that include, but are not limited to, exercise training, 
education, and behaviour change, designed to improve 
the physical and psychological condition of people with 
chronic respiratory disease and to promote the long-term 
adherence to health-enhancing behaviours

## Page 24

Pulmonary 
rehabilitation
Key intervention in GOLD guideline for COPD
One of the management with strongest evidence base
Latest research suggested early PR post discharge reduces re-
admission rate by almost 50%
Often as mandatory intervention prior to transplant
Has own BTS guidelines and within NICE guidelines
Mainly consist of COPD patient, but also extended to ILD and 
asthma patients in a lot of services

## Page 25

Pulmonary 
rehabilitation
Consists of
8-12 weeks of at least bi-weekly exercise sessions.
Mix of upper and lower limb exercises.
Mix of cardiovascular and strengthening exercises.
Educational component 
Hospital-based vs Virtual
