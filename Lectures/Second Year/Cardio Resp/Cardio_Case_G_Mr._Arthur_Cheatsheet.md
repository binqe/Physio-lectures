I Breathing Exercise: Pursed-Lip Breathing (PLB) + Paced Breathing
• Breathe in through the nose for
• 2 counts. Breathe out slowly through pursed lips (as if cooling hot
• soup) for 4 counts. Aim to reduce resting RR to 12–16 breaths/min. Use
• during all activities that provoke breathlessness.
• Practise supine with knees supported — eliminates postural demand.
