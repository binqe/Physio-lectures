I PATHOPHYSIOLOGY (KEY POINTS)
• Fletcher's 40 pack-year history is the dominant aetiological driver.
• Emphysematous destruction causes loss of elastic recoil .
• Without the elastic tethering that normally holds airways open, dynamic airway collapse occurs during expiration — gas is trapped distally.
• Repeated air trapping leads to chronic hyperinflation — the barrel chest.
