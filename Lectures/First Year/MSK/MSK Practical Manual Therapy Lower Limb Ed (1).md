Joint Mobilisations 
Joint mobilizations may be used to reduce pain and change muscle. For 
example, anterior knee pain often involves the dysfunction of the 
quadriceps muscle group and the patellofemoral joint. Treatment to the 
muscle will affect the joint, and, similarly, treatment to the joint will 
affect the muscle.

Myofascial Release (MFR)/ Trigger Point (TrP) 
A trigger point is defined as a focus of hyperirritability of a muscle and is 
thought to contribute to muscle tightness. On palpation of the muscle, 
an area of local tightness with tenderness, possible muscle twitch and 
referral of pain in a typical pattern is found. Having identified this, 
treatment involves applying manual pressure over the area. 
 
Application for practical: 
1. Test hip lateral rotation first (then retest after MFRs).  
2. Locate Piriformis, apply NMR/MFR/TrP 
3. Locate Glut Medius, apply NMR/MFR/TrP 
4. Retest hip lateral rotation.  
Foam Rollers, Massage Balls – SMR using principles from NMR, TrP 
Self-myofascial Release (SMR) for calf muscles (gastroc-soleus), and 
vastus lateralis.
