## Slide 22

- iliofemoral ligament
- pubofemoral ligament
- ischiofemoral ligament
- Anteriorly situated
- Originates at the AIIS and then fans out to insert along the intertrochanteric line of the femur.
- Resists hip extension, adduction (superior fibres), and abduction (inferior fibres).
- Anteriorly situated.
- Originates from the anterior aspect of the pubic ramus and the iliopubic eminence and inserts on the anterior surface of the intertrochanteric fossa.
- Resists hip abduction and extension.
- Posteriorly situated.
- Originates on the posterior surface of the acetabular rim and is said to "wind" around the joint and insert on the superior, anterior aspect of the greater trochanter.
- Resists hip extension and medial rotation.

## Slide 43

- Function of the q-angle
- The Q angle represents the relationship and alignment between the pelvis, leg and foot
- Line of force of the quadriceps muscle
- Important in athletes since repetitive stress on structures causes injury and pain
- Angle 13° - 20° ‘normal’. Wide discrepancies!
- Males lower end, females higher end
- Abnormally high Q angle can promote injuries at knee, hip, foot, and lower back
