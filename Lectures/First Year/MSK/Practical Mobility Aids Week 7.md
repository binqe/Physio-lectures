Session Introduction  
 
1. Why use a mobility aid?  
• Weight bearing (Pain and/or pathology) - patient may be 
▪ Non-weight bearing (NWB) 
 
▪ Touch weight-bearing (TWB) 
▪ Partial weight bearing (PWB) 
▪ Full weight bearing (FWB)   
• Balance - ageing, neurological, weakness, obesity   
• Weakness - lower limbs, fatigue   
• Confidence  
  
 
2. Types of walking aids: 
• Crutches: 
o Axillary 
o Elbow 
o Gutter 
• Stick:  
o Wooden/Metal 
o Tripod 
o Quadrupod 
• Frame:  
o Fixed 
o Wheeled.

3. SAFETY ISSUES  
A      Walking Aids:  
• Check intact structure, Ferrules, Strength, adjustability  
• Look for signs of damage: dents, bends, cracks, rusting, erosion of rubber, etc  
  
  
  
B  Patient    
• Medical condition, blood pressure, recent surgery, blood loss, etc  
• Cognitive abilities, joint range of motion, muscle strength, balance  
  
  
  
C  Environment  
 
• Clear of obstacles, dry, flat, may need to adapt walking to environment  
• Progress to normal situation  
  
  
  
4. Measurement of walking aid to patient:  
• Greater trochanter to 2 inches lateral to foot.   
• ASIS to lateral malleolus   
• ASIS to medial malleolus   
• Elbow at 30 degrees flexion, ulnar styloid to floor   
• 2 fingers below axilla to lateral border of foot (axillary crutches only)
