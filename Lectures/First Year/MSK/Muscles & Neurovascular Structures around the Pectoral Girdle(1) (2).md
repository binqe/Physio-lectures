## Page 2

Learning Objectives
• By the end of this session, students should be able to:
1.Identify the major muscles of the pectoral girdle and arm, 
including their origins, insertions, and functions.
2.Describe the innervation and vascular supply of these muscles, 
correlating them with their respective nerves and arteries.
3.Apply knowledge of muscle function and neurovascular supply to 
clinical assessments, injury evaluation, and rehabilitation 
strategies.
