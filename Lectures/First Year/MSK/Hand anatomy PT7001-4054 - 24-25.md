## Slide 4

- FUNDAMENTALS OF HAND ANATOMY
- Hand anatomy is easiest to learn by logically progressing through the material
- Bones → joints → movements at joints → bony landmarks → origin/insertion/action of muscles → neurovascular supply
- Similar to the foot, which has a dorsal and plantar aspect, the hand has a dorsal and palmar aspect

## Slide 5

- HAND FUNCTION
- Exceptionally refined complex needed for grip and manipulation e.g. dressing, playing instruments, writing, typing, etc.
- Balance of power and precision required, also, e.g. weight lifting, rock climbing, etc.

## Slide 11

- BONES AND JOINTS
- NINETEEN JOINTS!
- x five CARPOMETACARPAL (CMC) joints – the joint between the carpals and metacarpals
- x five METACARPOPHALANGEAL (MCP) joints – the joint at the base of the finger
- x four PROXIMAL INTERPHALANGEAL (PIP) joints – the joint in the middle of the finger
- x four DISTAL INTERPHALANGEAL (DIP) joints – the joint closest to the fingertip
- x one INTERPHALANGEAL joint – the joint in the middle of the thumb

## Slide 12

- JOINT MOVEMENTS OF THE FINGERS
- CARPOMETACARPAL (CMC) joints produce little movement
- METACARPOPHALANGEAL (MCP) joints produce;
- Flexion
- Extension
- Abduction
- Adduction
- PROXIMAL INTERPHALANGEAL (PIP) joints and DISTAL INTERPHALANGEAL (DIP) joints produce;
- Flexion
- Extension

## Slide 13

- PULLEY SYSTEM OF THE FINGERS
- The fingers possesses a complex pulley system to co-ordinate flexion of the digits consisting of:
- Long flexor tendon
- Annular pulleys
- Cruciate pulleys
- This system ensures the flexor tendons remain close to the joints axis of motion and prevent ‘bowstringing’
- A synovial sheath ensures:
- Smooth gliding of the tendons
- Synovial fluid
- Mechanical advantage

## Slide 14

- JOINT MOVEMENTS OF THE THUMB
- In the anatomical position, the thumb is rotated 90° medially to the rest of the digits, consequently, movements of the thumb are defined at medially orientated right angles to the movements of the other digits of the hand
- CARPOMETACARPAL (CMC) joint produces;
- Flexion
- Extension
- Abduction
- Adduction
- Opposition
- METACARPOPHALANGEAL (MCP) and the INTERPHALANGEAL joint produce;
- Flexion
- Extension

## Slide 15

- PULLEY SYSTEM OF THE THUMB
- The thumb possesses a pulley system too consisting of:
- Long flexor tendon
- Annual pulleys
- Oblique pulley

## Slide 16

- LIGAMENTS
- Large number of intercarpal ligaments!
- Both superficial and deep intermetacarpal ligaments on the palmar and dorsal aspect
- Collateral ligaments on between the phalanges
- Volar plate ligaments span the PIP on the palmar aspect of the hand to prevent excessive extension

## Slide 18

- PALMAR FASCIA
- The palmar fascia is a triangular-shaped fascial structure with its apex pointed proximally and the base of the triangle at the metacarpophalangeal joints
- It is composed of four longitudinal bands overlying each digit
- It is liable to undergo contraction, producing an inconvenient hereditary deformity known as ‘Dupuytren’s contraction’. The ring and little fingers are most frequently implicated, but the others may also be involved. The proximal phalanx is flexed and cannot be straightened, and the middle and distal phalanges become similarly flexed as the condition advances
