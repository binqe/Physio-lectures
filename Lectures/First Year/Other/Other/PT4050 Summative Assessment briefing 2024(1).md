# Summative Assessment briefing 2024

Applied Sciences for Physiotherapy Practice (PT4050)
Assessment Briefing: Biopsychosocial Essay
2024/25 Academic Year
Module learning outcomes:
At the end of this module, students will be able to:
Demonstrate an understanding of the components of human anatomy, physiology and biomechanics including the dynamic relationships of human structure and function as related to the lower limb and respiratory systems.
Demonstrate knowledge of social sciences concepts to needed to identify dimensions of human functioning that may be affected in individuals with dysfunction including the social and cultural influences on the health of individuals and populations.
Explain the effects of pathological processes underlying common clinical conditions seen in contemporary practice.
Demonstrate knowledge of the biopsychosocial model to make decisions about health and functioning in individuals and populations.
Summative unseen written examination
This will be a 2-hour paper focused on assessing your knowledge and understanding of human anatomy, physiology, behavioural and clinical science underlying Physiotherapy practice. It is aimed at exploring the breadth of knowledge taught in this module. It will contribute 100% of the module mark and assess LO 1, 2, 3 & 4.  It will have two sections A and B.
Section A: Multiple choice questions covering anatomy and physiology; you will be expected to answer all questions in this section.
Section B: Short answer questions covering anatomy, physiology, and Sociology components; you will have THREE questions in each knowledge area and required to answer TWO questions from each.
If you require additional information about this assessment, contact the module lead. Ngozi Onuegbu PhD  (n.onuegbu@londonmet.ac.uk).
