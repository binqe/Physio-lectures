# Chronic Stroke Case Study Treatment

Case Study (Treatment)
You are a community physiotherapist and Anthony, a 61-year-old male, has been referred to you by his general practitioner (GP).
Reason for referral
Anthony has had three falls in the last two months. He has not sustained any injury but needed help to get off the floor.
Presenting condition
Stroke 6 months ago: left middle cerebral artery thrombus with right sided weakness and language difficulties. After a week’s stay on the hyper-acute stroke unit, he had 11 weeks of bed-based rehabilitation. He was discharged home with the supported discharge team for ongoing rehabilitation for a further 6 weeks.
Social History
Lives in a third floor flat with lift – once inside all one level
Married to David
Works as a graphic designer but still on sick leave
Brother and sister live in New Zealand.
Large network of friends.
Currently housebound
Needs assistance with bathing, grooming, dressing.
Independent toileting, eating and drinking.
Previous exercise / activity – walking the dog and cycling to work.
Previous leisure activities – socialising with friends, bars, restaurants and going to the theatre.
Subjective assessment
Antony described the three falls as all being due to catching his right foot when walking indoors. On each occasion, he needed help to get up but only sustained bruising. Antony can walk around indoors using a stick in his left arm. He finds walking difficult, it is slow, and he has to concentrate hard not to catch his right foot when stepping. He is anxious about having more falls.
Objective assessment
General observation: In sitting; contracture in right arm, right shoulder girdle depression. Appeared alert, well kempt, some difficulties in expressive language during the conversation.
Assessment:
Questions
Make a problem list from the objective assessment and categorise them into impairments, activity limitations and participation restrictions
Identify a list of treatment priorities for Mr Anthony.
Select appropriate Physiotherapy interventions you will consider for managing Anthony
Who might you involve in the management of Anthony in the community
What are the health risks associated with long term prognosis of Anthony and identify the health promotion strategies you will recommend to these risks.
